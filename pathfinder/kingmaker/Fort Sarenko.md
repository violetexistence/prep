## F1 Gate / Entrance
>*The palisade rises out of the scrub like a splintered cliff, its timbers dark with rain and age. Between two squat corner towers, a drop-gate is shut tight like a jaw; its iron teeth. rest in the earth, and somewhere beyond, rope and wood complain with a slow, tired groan that comes and goes with the wind.*

**Force Open** (master) The winch in the north gatehouse must be repaired to raise the gate.
**Climb** (expert or trained with rope) Palisades are 15 feet high at their lowest points
**Search** There is a tiny breach in the south wall (river side) → trained Acrobatics to squeeze through (1 minute) → access to interior stock pen

## F2 Courtyard
>*The yard opens under the broken ring of battlements, a shallow bowl of trampled earth gone soft with leaves and silt. Boardwalks lace the upper walls in places, their planks bowed and lashed fast, bolstered over seasons; when the air shifts, they answer with a faint creak.*
>
>*Nearest the gate, in the east yard, a firepit’s ash has bled into the soil, charcoal scattered where the rain couldn’t quite wash it clean. The stock pen’s rails still stand under a sagging cover against the south wall. The benches and tables of a refectory are visible within a portico against the north palisade. Steps outside the portico lead up the parapets.
>
>*One of the boardwalks cuts across the courtyard, forming a kind of bridge connecting the north and south palisades. On the other side of the bridge, in the west yard, a small shrine of worked stone sites near the hall’s entrance, its clean lines dulled by dust. Garden beds lie swallowed by weeds, which threaten to swallow nearby steps leading up to the south palisade as well. A forge lies cold under cover against a stone watchtower, poking up out of the boardwalk and fastened to the tower beside it. The groaning that once seemed to come and go with the wind, now echoes insistently from inside the tower.*

**Groaning** (or is it something else) can be heard from inside the watchtower in the northwest corner of the fort.
**East Yard** A shrine to Abadar stands in the to one side of the entrance to the great hall
**West Yard** The coals and scattered ash from a bonfire remain.
**Stock Pen** Located under cover across from the refectory, against the south palisade
**Garden** Located under partial cover across from the great hall, against the south palisade
**Forge** Located under cover against the west palisade
**Battlements** The fort’s walls and towers are fully ringed with battlements. The crenels are open 4 feet above the parapet, the merlons reach 6 feet.
**Wooden Catwalks** / Scaffolding Some parapets are accessible only via wooden scaffolding and ladders. These are still in good working order despite the fort’s ruinous state.


## F3 Refectory
> *In the shadow of eaves lies what remains of the fort’s refectory, tucked where the wind breaks and the worst of the rain can’t quite reach. A few long tables and benches still sit where they were left, their planks bowed and furred with rot, half-buried under leaves that have drifted in and settled like silt. Paths where feet walked the same path over and over are worn smooth in a way neither grass nor weed will ever fully reclaim.
> 
> Everything that once counted as stores—sacks, barrels, anything that could spoil or burn—has been picked clean. The work of teeth and time have left only things you can’t eat: dented plates, a ladle with its handle bent from being hooked over an edge a thousand times, and in the darkest corner a great cast-iron cook pot squats in the shade like a stubborn animal, its belly stained black where smoke used to lick it, its surface showing no sign of rust.*

**Investigate** (Forest Lore, Nature, Occultism) Resting among the leaves is fallen Paueliel leaf. Superstition holds that these softwood trees grow only in places close to the First World. Most lumberjacks won’t harvest them. This leaf came from the Paueliel growing in the graveyard.

**Investigate** (Cooking Lore, Crafting, Society) Astoundingly, the cook pot shows no sign of rust and seem impervious to moisture damage. It has been seasoned with the oil stashed in the roof beam. There are well worn tracks in the ground where the cook pot has been rolled out of the way to be stowed when not in use. It was not stowed before the fort was abandoned. Unless it is stowed the secret door to the north gatehouse cannot be opened.

**Search** A peg in one of the roof beams hides a secret cache. Inside a vial wrapped in waxed cloth is stashed. The vial contains quartermaster’s seasoning oil.

**Search** There is a hidden door on the east wall, behind the cookpot, that provides accesses the north gatehouse.

## F6 Watchtower
The sound—and it’s definitely not groaning, but maybe chirping—echoes from somewhere inside this structure. Dim light from arrow slits, sconces no torches. Scaffolding and ladders cling to the walls offering the only path to climb from the ground to a small redoubt at the top. The scaffolding is still safe despite groaning and cracking under load. It typically requires 6 actions of climbing to reach the redoubt. 

**Search** The groaning is coming from a body swaying in the rigging midway up the tower, with one balefully glowing yellow eye.

**Investigate** (Nature, Occultism, Warfare Lore)  The body is just straw stuffed inside a captain’s uniform (cap and sash). The sound as well as the glowing come from a giant lantern cricket that lives inside the officer’s head gourd. The cricket is an awakened animal. It’s scared, confused, and trying to call for help (groaning) but has not yet learned language.
