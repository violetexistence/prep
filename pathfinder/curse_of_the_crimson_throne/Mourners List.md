**Orren Halvek** Doesn't have family in the city that you know of. He is Aiuvarin (mixed elvish ancestry) and brings calm, grounded energy. He is quietly funny--the sort people find it easy to open to. If engaged at the funeral, he will share that he is sitting with anger—anger at the gods for not giving him more time to know Beryndr’n the person—and that he was an admirer of the late Dawnmother but that he wasn’t close to her the way others may have been. Making a good impression with Orren may encourage him to share a memory he carries. It happened during the latter days of the plague. He was moving through the side corridor, near the vestry, carrying something—linens, probably, he was always carrying linens then—when he heard Beryndr’n voice through a half-open door. *I’m sorry it was plague. You deserved something slower. You deserved time to be a bother about it.* There was no reply. The woman she was talking to was already dead. An older woman, someone’s grandmother, who had come five days prior and hadn’t been conscious for the last three.

**Father Abstalar Zantus** Ten years ago, Abstalar Zantus returned to his hometown. He was an acolyte at the temple before a falling out with his master–Ezakien Tobyn. Zantus left Sandpoint before the Late Unpleasantness and returned to find the temple burnt to the ground—Ezakien killed in the fire. At the time, he was hesitant to take over the position. The temple would need rebuilding—a big job for which Zantus felt too young and inexperienced. Brother Pelden knows Abstalar through his Pathfinder Society connections and invited him to the service. Pelden hopes that Father Zantus can offer guidance to the new Dawnmother of Korvosa’s Sarenites. If engaged on the topic, he will happily offer stories about Sandpoint and the dama unfolding there recently. If any PC *makes a decent impression*, he may admit to personal struggles—Zantus find it difficult to balance realizing his own vision for the temple with the syncretic needs of a polytheistic community, and more than a little bit of insecurity around his own worthiness. He envies the Dawnmother and the other clerics of Sarenrae here in Korvosa. He is also completely unaware of Korvosa’s own unpleasantness of late, seeing only vast resources (compared to his own) and grand monotheistic houses of worship. All Zantus has heard are rumors of minor rebellion after the death of Eodred and of  potential renewing conflict between Magnimar and Korvosa—which could affect neighboring Sandpoint.

**Damiel Ornelos** The grandson of the Academae’s Headmaster is here alone from his noble house to lend his support to the new Dawnmother. His lingering romantic feelings for her are paramount, although she has had little time or interest in him since the outbreak. Some will see his attendance as confirmation that Asmodeans in Korvosa are testing the waters of an alliance with the Sarenites. Most already believe that the crown is breaking with House Arabasti’s long standing tie to that faction. Damiel doesn’t give a damn about any of these implications. If engaged, he readily admits to his admiration of the Oracle herself, and believes that she will set a fresh and glorious path for the temple of Sarenrae. If you are close to Ahalya and you are successful at making an impression with him, Damiel may ask for your advice on the topic of marriage.
