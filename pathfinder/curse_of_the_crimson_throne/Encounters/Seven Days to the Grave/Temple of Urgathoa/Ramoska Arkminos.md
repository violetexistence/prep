**Resistances** Ramoska is immune to the influence of moral sermons. Any attempt to influence Ramoska with romance or sentiment has a -5 cirumstance penalty. He is not immune to love. In fact, if despite this penalty you succeed, the first such success awards x2 Influence Points.

**Weaknesses** Credible evidence of a cure for vampirism instantly brings Ramoska to Influence 4 or adds +2 to his current level, whichever is higher. Attempts at influencing Ramoska with honest offers of redemption are made with a +2 circumstance bonus.

---

**Background** Ramoska has been sent here by his lord Tiriac, the Conte of Varno (Ustalav), lent into the service of his Red Mantis allies. Ramoska is himself a centuries old alchemist and scholar of disease. His passion is driven by a personal quest to rid himself of vampirism. He wants nothing more than to die, but not until he is cured. He fears being undead will prevent his soul from some reward or reunion in the afterlife. Ruan is the latest in a series of disease-resistant Varisians on whom Ramoska has conducted experiments, hoping one of them will also be resistant to vampirism.

**Appearance** A bald cadaver-thin figure in a stained aprod and hood, with ears that taper too sharply and fingers a little too long for human comfort. His nose is collapsed, his teeth small but sharp, and his voice is unexpectedly refined—soft consonants, a scholar's cadence.

**Personality** Curious, fastidious, easily irritated by incompetence
