# Seven Scourges of Urgathoa
## Item 7

==catalyst== ==consumable== ==magical==
**Usage** applied while adjacent to its basin

---

Seven stone basins ring the chamber, each brimming with a different bodily fluid — **blood**, **milk**, **bile**, **phlegm**, **pus**, **sweat**, and **tears**. Their fumes mingle into a cloying incense of rot and sweetness. Her faithful claim these fluids drip eternally from the Pallid Princess herself, harvested as sacrament for rites of indulgence, disease, and blasphemy.

---

**Scourge Invocation** A creature may anoint themselves with a Scourge to empower an [[Act of Worship]] or [[Act of Condemnation]].
	**Treat a success as a critical success**
	**Treat a critical failure as a normal failure**
	**However**, any failure **immediately exposes you to Blood Veil**, skipping onset and proceeding directly to Stage 1 on a failed Fortitude save.

A creature cannot benefit from the **same Scourge more than once for Act of Worship**.  
Any Scourge used during **Act of Condemnation is defiled and becomes inert**, regardless of the check result.
