This sinister item functions like a robe of useful items, but specifically for the serious necromancer. It appears to be an unremarkable robe, but a character who dons it notes that it is adorned with small embroidered figures representing undead creatures. Only the wearer of the robe can see the embroidery, recognize them as the creatures they becomes, and detach them. One figure can be detached using the robe's activate activity. Detaching a figure causes it to becomes an actual undead creature (see the list below). The undead creature has the minion trait, meaning it can use 2 actions when you Command it. You can issue a Command for the current turn as part of the activation. The undead creature remains animated for 1 minute before collapsing to dust. You can have a maximum of four minions under your control.
