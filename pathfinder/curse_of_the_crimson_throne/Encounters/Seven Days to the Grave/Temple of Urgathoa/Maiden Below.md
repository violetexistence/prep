```pf2e-stats
# Maiden Below
## Hazard 7

---

==complex== ==haunt==
**Stealth** +18 (expert) (the statue appears dormant until triggered)
**Description** A gold-leafed woman stands waist-deep in crystalline water. Soft face, open arms, and a sunburst crown evoke a healer-saint—until the illusion slips. Below the navel she is bare bone, ribs like hooked teeth, pelvis flowering into rot. Her smile is warm but wrong. Eye sockets too deep. She raises a goblet; an invitation.

---

**Disable** DC 21 Diplomacy to perform an [[Act of Worship]], DC 25 Religion to perform an [[Act of Condemnation]], or [[Dispel Magic]] (rank 5, counteract DC 32) to counteract the haunt

---

**AC** 26, **Fort** +17, **Will** +15
**Hardness** 5, **HP** 80 (BT —); **Immunities** critical hits, object immunities, precision; **Weaknesses** holy 5  

---

**Consecrate** `[reaction]` **Trigger** A living creature anathema to Urgathoa crosses into area G14 (inner sanctum); **Effect** The inner sanctum east of the doors becomes **consecrated unholy** and the haunt rolls initiative. Worshippers of Urgathoa gain **[[Feast That Moans]]**. Creatures anathema to Urgathoa suffer **[[Face Fucked by Abstinence]]**.

---

**Routine** `[one-action]` The haunt channels unholy power into one worshipper of Urgathoa within the consecrated area. That creature gains **Put Out the Light** until the start of the haunt’s next turn or until the haunt is destroyed or disabled.

---

**Put Out the Light** `[reaction]` **Trigger** A divine spell is cast by a creature under **Face Fucked by Abstinence**; **Effect** The chosen worshipper makes a *counteract* check at rank 4 using **+16** or their own spellcasting modifier, whichever is higher.

---

**Reset** If disabled, the Maiden Below reforms at the next midnight.
```
