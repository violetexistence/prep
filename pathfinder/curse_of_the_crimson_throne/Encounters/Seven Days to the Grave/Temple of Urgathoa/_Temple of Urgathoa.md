## G1 Entry Hall
>The scuffed stone walls of this chamber have been plastered over and then decorated with lurid murals of skeletons cavorting among the dead of a Korvosa that has completely succumbed to plague. Simple wooden doors lead to the north, south, and west, each bearing a painting of a scythe-wielding skeleton. A sizable double door stands on the east wall, appearing in the mural as a massive set of double doors opening into the pyramid foundation of Castle Korvosa. Two more scythe-wielding skeletons decorate these large doors.

**Doors** Hardness 10, HP 40, BT 20, **Thievery DC 29**
**Death’s Breath Doors** Triggers when any creature who has not worshipped at the Maiden Below touches the door; **Stealth DC 26** (trained)

[[Death’s Breath Doors]]

**Creatures** None initially. If the Death’s Breath Doors trigger—or if combat breaks out here while the temple is *not yet on alert*—4x Gray Maidens from [[#G2 Guard Post]] investigate.

**On Alert** The Gray Maidens from area G2 redeploy here and take up a defensive position near the double doors, bows trained on the entry. Once they move, G2 is left empty; the Queen’s Physicians instead redeploy to the ground floor of [[#G8 The Blood Vats]].

## G5 Operating Room
>Eight unpleasant looking beds stand here. Their sharp iron frames are threaded with worn manacles and stained leather straps. Several are occupied by obviously unwilling patients; all are bound and in various states of consciousness, and their combined moans murmur throughout the room. Between them stand several small tables strewn with gore soaked pans, flasks of mysterious fluids, and all manner of cruel-looking cutting instruments. A sizable brown-crimson stain covers much of the eastern wall, as if all the blood from a body once held there had exploded forth in a single violent eruption.

Locked (average, DC 25 Thievery) iron doors (hardness 9, HP 36, BT 18) lead to [[#G6 Patient Cells]]. Each of the cultists in the complex carries keys to this door and the cells beyond. 

**Patients** include 1x recently deceased, 2x unconscious (a priest of Sarenrae named *Pelden Quince* and a Sable Company hostler named *Dalvun Krand*), and 3x actual [zombies](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=1919). 

**Creatures** 2x [[Cultist of Urgathoa]] and 2x [[Queen's Physician]].

**On Alert** The cultists redeploy to [[#G13 Hall of Pestilence]], and the Queen’s Physicians redeploy to the ground floor of [[#G8 The Blood Vats]].

## G8 The Blood Vats
> The stinging scent of harsh chemicals chokes this high-ceilinged chamber. Three huge metal vats bubble here, each more than six feet tall. A sturdy series of catwalks ten feet off the ground stretches over and around the vats, allowing those above to attend whatever slurry produces the foul green-brown mist emanating from each gigantic vessel. Circling the upper portion of the room is a mosaic of white, black, and green stone that depicts a giant half-skeletal woman in black veils dancing among fields of the dead, undead, and dying.

Doors to the **west**, **south**, and **east** lie on lower level.
Door to the **north** is **locked** (average, DC 25 Thievery) and can be opened by the key carried by **Arkminos**.

```pf2e-stats

---
```

**Creatures** 6x [[Cultist of Urgathoa]].

**On Alert** The cultists hold this chamber as a redoubt. Dr. Davaulus (after warning Lady Andaisin) joins them here. Rolth Lamm takes position atop the central catwalk. Five Queen’s Physicians relocate to the ground floor to defend the temple.

## G13 Hall of Pestilence
>The reek of burning wax wafts through this morbid chamber, with several tall, misshapen candles being the apparent source. Workspaces strewn with tall beakers of foul-colored liquids, parchments covered in insidious symbols, and cages of whimpering rodents fill large alcoves in both the northern and southern walls. A pair of huge stone doors hang ajar to the east, revealing a long hallway leading further into the dark. At the room’s center stand four large, cylindrical glass vats, each filled with a bubbling emerald fluid that tints the chamber’s light a noxious green. Within each suspension floats a malformed abomination—something part human, part angel, and part horse—things of half-formed muscle with dead, fleshless equine skulls. Three of the forms are motionless, but the fourth twitches now and then.

**Creatures** 2x [[Cultist of Urgathoa]] tend the tubes and defend the room. One living [leukodaemon](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=2893) is contained here; if released, it treats *no one* in the temple as an ally.

**On Alert** Eight priests of Urgathoa concentrate here to protect the chamber (including cultists redeploying from elsewhere in the complex).

Doors to the east lead to [[#G14 Inner Sanctum]].

**Tubes** These are arcane/alchemical contraptions that were used to trap the daemons, hardness 1, HP 8 (BT 4). DC 15 Religion (trained) to see these were not made by a divine magic practitioner and to learn more about these outsiders. DC 20 (trained) Occultism or Arcana to see these contraption combine the magic of true names, cold iron, and alchemical devices to imprison the creatures within.

3x dead daemons → their life was consumed in the rituals used to empower experiments in the plague known as *Blood Veil*.

1 living [leukodaemon](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=2893) → if released it will not treat any of the cultists as allies.
