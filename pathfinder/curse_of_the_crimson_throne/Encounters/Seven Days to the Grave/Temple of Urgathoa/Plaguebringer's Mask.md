## Item 5

A plaguebringer's mask grants +1 item bonus on saving throws against nauseating scents and immunity to one specific disease (chosen at the time of the mask's creation). The mask also veils the wearer's intentions. Anyone sensing motive, using thought detecting effects, or aura reading, will register the wearer as neutral for the situation.
