CITIZENS OF KORVOSA!

ENDRIN ISLE, ALSO KNOWN AS OLD KORVOSA, IS UNDER QUARANTINE!

BY THE POWERS OF THE OFFICES OF THE LORDS MAGISTRATE AND THE DUTIES ENTRUSTED TO THE OFFICE OF PUBLIC HEALTH, ON THIS THE TWENTY NINTH DAY OF ARODUS IN THE YEAR FOUR THOUSAND SEVEN HUNDRED SEVEN BY ABSALOM RECKONING, LET IT BE KNOWN:

NO PERSONS ARE TO ENTER OR LEAVE ENDRIN ISLE ON PENALTY OF DEATH! 

THIS ACTION IS TAKEN UNDER ADVISEMENT FROM THE QUEEN’S PHYSICIANS, DR. REINER DEVAULUS, AND THE ORACLE OF SARENRAE, THAT WE MIGHT PREVENT THE SPREAD OF INFECTION AND SAVE MORE LIVES FROM THE PLAGUE KNOWN AS BLOOD VEIL.

IF YOU BE BLOOD OF ARKONA, 
IF YOU BE A SABLE COMPANY MARINE SERVING IN FORT KORVOSA,
IF YOU BE A CADET TRAINING AT ENDRIN ACADEMY,

YOU HAVE ONE DAY TO DEPART ENDRIN ISLE. 

ALL OTHER CITIZENS NORTH OF THE NARROWS ARE BOUND TO SHELTER IN PLACE IMMEDIATELY. 

THE KORVOSAN GUARD SHALL SEAL OR REMOVE ALL ROADS CONNECTING ENDRIN ISLE AND THE REST OF KORVOSA. DO NOT ATTEMPT TO BREAK QUARANTINE. YOU WILL RECEIVE NO WARNING TO TURN BACK.

THIS ORDER WILL BE LIFTED AS SOON AS IT IS SAFE TO DO SO.

MAY THE GODS AND ALL THE LOST OMENS HAVE MERCY ON OUR SOULS.
