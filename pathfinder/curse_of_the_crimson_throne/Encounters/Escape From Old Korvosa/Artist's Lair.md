#### B1 Front Room
>*The smell in this entryway is of must and mildew, much of it coming from the mud tracked over the floorboards, as if a small army had marches through the room. To the south stands a single empty set of shelves.*

**DC 15 Survival, DC 20 Perception** The muddy prints on the floor were left by the emperor’s green when they invaded the building several days ago. There were fix of them and they dragged the unconscious body of Salvator out of the building a and took it with them.

#### B2 Bedroom
>*A single bed, the blankets and pillows atop it scattered and in disarray, sits to the south in this room. More muddy boot prints mar the wooden floor here and splash of dried blood decorates one pillow.*

**DC 15 Survival, DC 20 Perception** When they invaded, the emperor’s green found Salvator sleeping here. One of them knocked hi out with a blow to the head, and the others helped drag him back to Pilts’s Palace. The prints lead up to the bed.

#### B3 Salvator’s Studio
> Both of this room’s windows are tightly shuttered, yet the air inside seems strangely fresh and scented, no doubt because of the six large candles that burn within. Each candle has been affixed by a glob of melted wax to the crown of a gleaming polished skull, and each of these impromptu and grisly candleholders has been placed atop an otherwise clear desk to the west, arrayed in a gentle arc. A chair sits before the desk, and a careful stack of papers and scrolls sits inside the arc of skulls. To the south stands a nearly empty cabinet, its shelves barren save for a few paintbrushes and a cracked pottery urn.

#### B5 Sinkhole

**Creatures** [Ofalth](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=3117)

```pf2e-stats
# Dock-Mire Sinkhole
## Hazard 6
---
==complex== ==environmental==

**Stealth** +18 (expert) (or –10 and no minimum proficiency if the surface is disturbed)

**Description** A 15-foot-wide “solid” patch of dockside mud—black silt, rotting reed-mats, and floating refuse crusted over with scum—collapses underfoot. What looks like stable ground is actually a hungry sinkhole fed by river backwash and sewer seepage.

---

**Disable** DC 22 Survival (trained) to disturb the crust and collapse it safely from a distance (revealing the hazard and preventing it from becoming hidden again until it settles)

**Submerge** `[free-action]` **Trigger** A Huge or smaller creature walks onto the sinkhole; **Effect** The triggering creature drops into the mud up to its waist as the crust gives way with a wet *slurp*. The sinkhole rolls initiative if it hasn’t already.

---

**Routine** (1 action) The sinkhole drags down each creature within it. A creature submerged up to its waist becomes submerged up to its neck. A creature submerged up to its neck is pulled under and must hold its breath to avoid [suffocation](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2439). A creature in the sinkhole can attempt a DC 24 Athletics check to Swim in the sludge.
	**Success** If it’s submerged up to its neck or worse, it raises itself by one step on a. If it’s submerged only up to its waist, it can move 5 feet on a success.
	**Critical Failure** The creature is pulled down one step.

**Escape** A creature that Swims out of the sinkhole escapes the hazard and is prone in a space adjacent to the sinkhole’s edge.

**Aid** Other creatures can Aid the trapped creature (typically with a rope, pole, or hooked boatline), or attempt to haul them out with their own DC 24 Athletics check, using the same results as if the trapped creature attempted the check.

---

**Reset** The sinkhole still submerges anyone who walks into it, but the surface doesn’t become hidden again until it settles over the course of 24 hours (as silt, scum, and floating trash drift back into a deceptively “stable” crust).
```
