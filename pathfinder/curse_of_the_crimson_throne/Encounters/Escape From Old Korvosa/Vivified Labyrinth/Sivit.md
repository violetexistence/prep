**Claw Rake** `[three-actions]` Sivit rears back on her hind legs and makes two claw Strikes at the same target, using the same attack bonus as her highest melee attack. If both attacks deal damage, the target takes extra damage equal to one claw Strike.

**Infernal Wound** (divine) Sivit's claw Strike also deals 2d6 persistent bleed damage that resists attempts to heal it. The flat check to stop the bleeding starts at DC 20. The DC is reduced to 15 only if the bleeding creature or an ally successfully assists with the recovery. The DC to Administer First Aid to a creature with an internal wound is increased by 5. A spellcaster or item attempting to use healing magic on a creature suffering from an infernal wound must succeed at a DC 29 counteract check or the magic fails to heal the creature.

**Pounce** `[one-action]` Sivit Strides and makes a Strike at the end of that movement. If Sivit began this action hidden, she remains hidden until after the attack.

**Warding Glyph** Once per week, Sivit can create a magical symbol as though casting a heightened glyph of warding spell. Sivit usually shapes the glyph to take the form of a fiendish symbol, and attunes it to deactivate in the presence of an Unholy creature. A creature without this trait and not being escorted by one must succeed at a DC 29 Will save or suffer one of the following spell effects, chosen by Sivit when creating the symbol: synaptic pulse (5th), charm (4th), fear (3rd), phantom pain (3rd), sleep (3rd).
