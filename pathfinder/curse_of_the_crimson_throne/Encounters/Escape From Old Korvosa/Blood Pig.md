```pf2e-stats
# Grab Pig `[one-action]`
---
==attack== ==blood pig==

**Requirements** You have at least one free hand

You attempt to grab a pig within your reach. You can also attempt to keep or improve your hold on a pig you already grabbed. Make a DC 15 Athletics check.
**Critical Success** The pig is restrained. At the end of your next turn it squirms loose and becomes grabbed instead.
**Success** The pig is grabbed.
**Failure** You drop the pig.
```

```pf2e-stats
# Throw Pig `[one-action]`
---
==attack== ==blood pig==

You attempt to throw a pig at a target. If your target is 10 feet away or less, you automatically succeed. Otherwise, make an Athletics check against DC 15 + 1 per additional 5 feet thrown.

**Success** You hit your intended target. They may attempt to Catch Pig as a reaction.
**Failure** The pig is thrown to a random space adjacent to your intended target.
**Critical Failure** The pig is thrown to a random space within 20 feet of your intended target.

```

```pf2e-stats
# Catch Pig `[reaction]`
---
==attack== ==blood pig==

**Trigger** You are targeted with a thrown pig or a pig is thrown through a square you currently threaten.
**Requirements** You have at least one free hand but can release anything you are holding as part of this reaction.

---

Attempt a DC 11 Athletics check to catch the pig.

**Critical Success** You catch the pig, it squeals, and is grabbed.
**Success** You catch the pig and it is grabbed.
**Failure** You catch the pig and it is grabbed, but immediately rolls on the Squirming Pig table.
**Critical Failure** You drop the pig.
```
