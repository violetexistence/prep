Ahalak, sensual male hunter, things the rotten food thrown at the group is actually quite tolerable, once you brush off the mud. He was the one who killed the first restless dead. He is afraid to die, but more afraid of seeing shame in One Life’s (his father) eyes when he returns home.

Hargev, curious male hunter, hid a cache of supplies, including a crate of salt pork, barrels of wheat grain, a cold cellar stacked with sacks of root vegetables, and a chord of split firewood, in the attic above a glasses maker (Aolifu’s) in Ankar-Te. He desperate wants to some passerby’s attention and offer to buy the Boneslayers’ freedom using the cache.

Nalmid, deceitful female acolyte, has not received visions and desperately wants to be an oracle, like her big sister Words from Dust. Unfortunately, she is very good a reading people, and telling them what they want to hear. She is the only member of the group that speaks Taldane (common).

Words from Dust (*Shadfrar*), insightful female Oracle of Bones, wants to understand the secrets of the human body. She is current on something of fad diet she invented involving ant toxin.

**FORESHADOW** A single parent and child are shopping in meatmarket. The parent wears a death’s head talisman to command their undead servant, who is being tasked with carrying a huge burden of supplies. They are struggling to properly use the talisman before the child shows them how. 

The four boneslayers are sent by Thousand Bones to Kaer Maga on a mission to bring the shama’s allies safety to the Kallow Mounds to meet with him. Unfortunately, they arrived a few days before the people they were to meet. In that time, they have managed to commit some crimes in Ankar-Te. Over objections (mostly from Words from Dust), the group wandered out into the ring. They were told about Thrown Bones, supposedly the most amazing gaming house. On the way, they came upon a palanquin being born by “Twice Born”. Ahalek, upon realizing what “Twice Born” meant, did as the young Boneslayer was taught, and destroyed them. The Palanquin’s occupant was Horus Ilaktya, illustrious necromancer and proprietor of Last Rites — a business that sells custom-created undead as servants and mounts.

Horus oversees Ankar-Te along with High Priestess Shamalay Kasan. He cannot allow such public prejudice against his trade to stand, especially in his own backyard. He has placed the Shoanti in crow’s cages and hung them up on the street outside the Palace of the Child Goddess, where they are guaranteed to see the most foot traffic. Horus doesn’t care about the fate of the criminals. He does care about the fear and respect given him by his neighbors. To that end, he has placed a sign near the cages that reads, “Assaulted Ilkatya.” He has so far been gratified—the Shoanti’s pleas for mercy are yet to overcome the populace’s fear of the local necromancer.

#### Negotiation
DC 30 Diplomacy to make a deal for the Boneslayers’ freedom.
**Critical Success** As success, but Horus only demands one knuckle.
**Success** Horus agrees to let the criminals free as long as one of them states publicly in common that “an assault on the twice-born is an assault on the owner”, and 3 knuckles from the hand that struck him.
**Failure** No deal. Can try again, +Tension
**Critical Failure** Offense taken! Cannot try again.

**Reward** For finding such amicable terms, the necromancer sends them away with his compliments and a token of his friendship, an amulet containing **Scarab Dust**. 
**Development** Negotiating with someone who not only creates but then sells restless dead does not sit well (**-1 Respect Points**) with the Boneslayers. They are not universally upset with the PCs. Hargev and Nalmid are just happy to be free. Ahalak is also grateful but fears what his father will think when he hears of his foolishness and the actions the Tshamek took just to save them from it.

#### Break Out
DC 33 Thievery to break the Boneslayers out of their cages. 5 Freedom Points (FP) required.
**Critical Success** +2 FP
**Success** +1 FP
**Failure** No progress, +1 Tension
**Critical Failure** +2 Tension

**Development** Caught! A cry goes up and a mob of concern citizens arrest the PCs (yea right—roll initiative). In 3 rounds, a [[Death's Head Company|death’s head security company]] arrives with some kind of murder golem (purchased from the Ardocs). This company is composed of necromancers whose official job is to destroy any twice-born that show signs of disobedience, defect, or the worst condition—sentience. They also repossess twice-born whose owners have missed payments to Horus. In reality, these are the enforcers of Ankar-Te.
