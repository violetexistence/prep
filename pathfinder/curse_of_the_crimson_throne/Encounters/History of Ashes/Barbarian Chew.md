The Shoanti of the Cinderlands combine lime, crimson galtroot leaves, and some say horse blood to create this invigorating chew. Aside from staining the teeth a bloody shade, barbarian chew causes the user to feel stronger, but also angrier.
  
The save for addiction to barbarian chew is DC 14, but the maximum addiction stage of barbarian chew never progresses beyond stage 1.  
  
**Saving Throw** DC 14 Fortitude; **Onset** 10 minutes; **Maximum Duration** 8 hours; **Stage 1** ignore the effects of fatigue (10 minutes); **Stage 2** ignore the effects of fatigue and increase the additional damage from Rage by 1 (1 hour); **Stage 3** stupefied 1 (1 hour)
