Roll 1d20 once per days, at dawn. Add a second roll if the party travels at night.

**Escalation Die** Add +1 to the roll for each consecutive day you have been more than 2 hexes from water. Resets when you resupply.

**Hydrology** — distance from the nearest blue line sets your DCs
**Geography** — which region you’re in sets which table to roll on.

Rules: [Environment](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2768)

- `!` Not a fight. Do not roll initiative unless the players force it.

**Mirages** During one of the party’s attempts to seek water, spring this encounter on them. Instead of rolling in the open, turn the survival check as well as any checks made to aid the primary character into secret checks. Regardless of the result, the primary character believes they have found a path to water. After some time, doubt creeps in whether the water is real. It should have been found by now. Letting hexploration activities spent on seeking water go might be difficult. Don’t make this a mechanics discussion, though. This is an RP opportunity. How do the PCs express their doubts? How do they express support for each other? How do they handle their judgement being questioned, especially under survival pressure? **Reward** Characters who express their doubts honestly and feel heard take a +1 circumstance bonus forward to saving throws. Characters who listen to doubts and either change their course or proceed with the support of those expressing doubt take a +1 circumstance bonus forward to their exploration activities.

**Dry Spring** The water source marked on the map turns out to be dry. Use this encounter when the PCs arrive at their destination and expect to refresh their supply. DC 20 Nature reveals a recent tremor has shifted the water table, draining this spot. DC 25 Survival (or a critical success with Nature) can reveal minimal water is still present in nearby plants/roots and can be retrieved with effort (requires an individual hexploration activity). If the additional activity is undertaken, apply the results of the original Survival check and use these outcomes in place of those described in subsist.
**Critical Success** You find enough water for two people.
**Success** You retrieve enough water for one person.
