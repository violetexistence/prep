>After many days upon the road north, at last you arrive at the great cataracts that come pouring down the Storval Rise, a thousand-foot-tall cliff that slices Varisia nearly in half and separates the fertile lowlands from the rough and craggy Storval Plateau.
>
>Several barges are moored at piers on east bank of the river. A line of travelers is heading east on foot, taking the long, steep road that follow the Yondabakari up through the narrow crack the river cuts in the cliff wall. A few brace souls even portage canoes and other small boats up the trail. A smaller, less traveled road breaks away and trails northeast along the bottom of the Rise. 
>
> The trail terminates in an open field nestled up against the cliff face. Several corrals, hitching posts, and other amenities stand in a rough circle. A small congregation of traveling merchants sit queued up in a line at the clearing’s edge. In the center of the clearing, a huge set of bronze double doors stands embedded in the rock face, the portals embossed with strange runes.

The merchants queued are the last to be admitted today. There are only three Duskwardens working the Halflight Path right now and each will escort no more than twelve at a time. The line is composed of exactly 36 souls — no room for more. The rest of the travelers seeking passage have retired to the pavilion for the evening for dinner and merry making.

>Outside of the clearing a colorful pavilion is pitched next to several equally colorful wagons. Music can be heard from inside the pavilion as well as laughter and singing. Over the entrance, a weathered sign hangs.

The wagons and pavilion are part of the Giuleppeschi caravan, wandering entertainers composed mainly, but not entirely, of Varisians. They will remain camped here until the queue into Kaer Maga drains, at which time they will make their way up the main trail, and continue on the Cinder Road. In the meantime, they offer hospitality to those waiting to enter the City of Strangers, including food, music, and are happy to exchange their stories about The Hex and The Cinderlands for any involving Korvosa’s new queen.

> Inside the door, an owl perches on lip of a barrel of delicious smelling wine. 
> 
> To the right a family of halflings is tucking into a large savory pie, father helping the youngest place a napkin into their shirt collar.
> 
> Ringing the pole in the center of the pavilion are a trio of musicians playing the saltarello, while a fourth with bluish gold skin dances gracefully around the other three.
> 
> Tucked beneath an enormous hanging spider plant, with her knees up bracing a sketchbook, a spectacled woman’s eyes glitter as she sips her wine, staring thoughtfully at her drawing before erasing something, and then gratifyingly replacing it.
> 
> Laughing, a pregnant couple wearing all their possessions, stumble into the center and join the dancer attempting to follow their movements, and failing much to everyone’s good-natured amusement.
> 
> Behind the musicians in a communal table and benches with a selection of folk, talking, toasting, and planning. A serving boy scuttles about the communal table offering to take used glasses and plates.
> 
> Across from them is a smaller table with a trio of men with big appetites, who keep trying to get the serving boy’s attention whenever he passes by.

**What’s in the delicious smelling barrel?**
- mulled wine
- floating star anis, fangberries, and orange slices, all stained red
- **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** Suppresses the effects of fatigue for one hour as long as you remain within the pavilion.

**What’s with the owl?**
- It’s real—just very still. Name is Io (after Iomedae)
- It understands common and can speak, though it doesn’t with strangers.
- Io is the Shalaye’s familiar. She watches the entrance for troublemakers and alerts Shalaye to any issues; Perception +21

**What’s hanging over the entrance?**
- A pair of harrow cards stuck like daggers into shield
- The shield is old, device faded, looks Chellish
- Cards: The Wanderer, The Mute Hag (upside down)

**Who is the family eating savory pie?**
- ⚠ These are the **Boram** (dad), **Etune** (mom), **Kaleb**, **Rillka**, **Yamyra**, and **Jamir** (napkin), hillock halflings from Sandpoint.
- They have a pet kid goat, **Cayden** (after Cayden Cailean).
- **Boram** (*caring male halfling courier*) used to deliver mail on the Magnimar to Galduria run
- **Etune** (*sad female halfling plumber*) the family is moving because of an offer from Cliffside Millworks in the Cavalcade. Etune carries a letter of recommendation from Lady Kendra Deverin (recently elected Mayor of Sandpoint), for whom Etune worked as a maid this last year. Trade positions for halflings on the Lost Coast are scarce due to [racism] entrenched leadership and lifetime positions.

**What on the menu?**
- **Journey’s End Rolls** (End of Thyme Rolls) A pastry pocket stuffed with filling made of mushroom, sweet potato, onion, thyme, clove, and cinnamon braised cabbage.
- **Mr. Beaver’s River Trout** River trout dusted in seasoned flour, seared in butter, and served with a lemon-shallot pan sauce and chunks of buttered hearth bread.
- **Turtleback Ferries** Mix of dates, hazelnuts, cinnamon, honey, and blackberries piled into scooped out pear halves, topped with a dab of butter, and roasted. Serviced with a dab of yogurt.
- **Drinks** Boulderhead Bock, Longbeard Lambic, Fort Indros Ale, The mulled wine in the barrel is mixed from a Galdurian red (‘05), Irrisen Icewine, and Shalaye has a bottle or two of Korvosan wine for those who wish to toast Her Majesty. She also keeps a bottle of Daggermark Drip for medicinal uses. For those who enjoy beverages without spirit, she has mixed a pitcher of *Not-Bog-Grog*, a non-alchoholic approximation of the infamous Wartle concoction—(soft) orange juice cut with honey, salt, sharpened with ground galtroot, and turned swamp-gas green with pressed lichen syrup.

**Who is the dancer?**
- **Esa** (shy nonbinary human/varisian kapenia dancer), they are cousin to Seoni
- Their skin is not actually gold or blue at all. It’s actual dark—like coffee—and covered in glittering tattoos. 
- Full back piece—scarab with wings spread, grasping two rune; one in its forelegs (Thullax, corrosion); one in its hind (Piteregrin, transposition)
- Show skillful (or at least unambiguous/direct) interest and they will have a drink with you. They can tell you about _sihedron_ (aka [The Shattered Star](https://pathfinderwiki.com/wiki/Sihedron)), a powerful artifact created by the archmage Xin.

**Who is the sketchbook?**
- ⚠ **Halissa Jeggare** (ne Vekker, _independent female human explorer_)
- She’s traveling to the Great Library (more formally The Therassic Spire) to complete her *Survey of Hydrology in Varisia*. Halissa can tell players where to find the Spire in Highside Stacks as well as where the safest places to stay while visiting. 
> *Don’t stay in the Core. There’s a little temple—dragon spirit—I think. My Tian is spotty. On the Street of Little Gods just down from Gadka’s. Tell them you’re with Vekker. They don’t advertise but they will give you a room that’s quiet and cheap.*
- She believes there is a vast source of water hidden in subterranean rivers and that these may be partially the cause of the Cinderlands’ famous storms. She wants to be published (*respect*), of course, but ultimately she dreams of transforming the Cinderlands into a paradise.

**Hey, should I tip anyone? No one is asking for money.**
- Yes, you should. On closer inspection there are cups and hats set out for just this sort of generosity.
- If PCs contribute appropriately, they are welcomed and treated generously in kind.
- If they don’t, they are welcomed anyway.
- **Shalaye’s Edicts** aid fellow travelers, find what life has to offer
- **Shalaye’s Anathema** demand payment for hospitality, allow trolls to ruin the vibe

**Who dat preggers couple?**
- ⚠ **Doram** (depressed male aiuvarin aristocrat)
- ⚠ **Wessa** (capricious female human muse)
- They have been run out of Magnimar for defying the wishes of Doram’s family. They hold hands constantly out both love and fear. They heard that anyone is welcome in Kaer Maga and decided to stake everything on moving there.
- One name, Elias Sayer, was given to them. It’s rumored that as long you’re either pretty or strong, he’s got work for you and he takes care of his boys. Doram plans to look him up and get work while Wessa carries their child.

**Who’s all at the communal table?**
- **Rek Zechnas** (self-absorbed male gnomish inventor) is drunker than usual and having a marvelous time testing his latest construct, a table-golem adept at passing butter, salt, or any other condiment. He hopes to sell the table-golem pattern to the Ardocs to fund his research into a new pattern: a hand crossbow that shoots teleportation circles.
- ⚠ **Nessa Dorn** (selfless female human priest) is a retired soldier turned lay priest of Cayden Cailean. She is traveling with her friend Ash to recruit adventurers. When she thinks people aren’t paying attention, she is putting her own coin into the house’s cups for other patrons. Nessa can warn the PCs about the “Twice Born” and knows that the biggest creator of such abominations is in Ankar-Te.
- ⚠ **Corvine “Ash” Deggan** (daring male human mercenary) is headed up the Halflight Path to see Black Marin, who runs a fighting academy in The Bottoms. Ash is recruiting a few specialists for an assault on Fort Rannick to take back Turtleback Ferry from the stone giants and their lamia commander. He knows Warden Abra Lopati (a former brother-in-arms) lost his wife to some kind of incursion from the Undercity.
- **Ymeri Kane** (amoral nonbinary human broker) a native of The Hex, is returning home with a wagon full of goods best not itemized. Sharp, amusing, and entirely without scruple. Kane is an incredible source of information, if you can get them to divulge anything of value in anything other than double speak (think Garrick from DS9). Factions, politics, local customs, you name it. Probably an agent of the Commerce League.
- ⚠ **Glib Writher** (*insufferable male human lawyer*) is an Asmodean cleric from Korvosa specializing in generational pacts and curses who has been sent for by Lord Victae Cobaru. He does not know the exact need for which his services have been retained, but has been provided with a long list of indemnities, including prolonged sunlight deprivation, minor blood loss, and bodily contact with the undead. He is terrified but cannot pass up the large commission offered by Lord Cobaru.

**Who is the serving boy?**
- Pim (bright eyed male human orphan)!
- He is going up the Halflight Path in the morning.
- He’s been working here for the Caravaners, bussing tables, and doing other odd jobs to pay the Duskwardens. He thinks the fee is 1 gp, which he should have after tonight’s tips. It’s not; it’s 2 gp. That’s okay though, because Shalaye secretly intends to match his earnings to pay his fare.
- Pim is going to study at the Lyceum and star at the Lakeside and become famous.
- He can quietly listened to all the travelers coming and going. As a result he’s learned a fair bit about the situation in the Oriat. Specifically, he knows about the war between the Brothers of the Seal, and how Father Zho will soon put an end to the traitorous Scions, led by Aldair Aemon the “faithless”. Aemon’s turf is marked with red sashes. Avoid them.

**Who is running this place anyway?**
- **Shalaye Giuleppeschi** (*wise female human/varisian chef*)
- Like many of her kin, Shalaye follows the laws of Desna.
- Her true passion is in feeding people and making them comfortable. She drives the crimson coast road, setting up her caravan anywhere travelers gather in spaces not regulated by any of the major settlements.
- Working with her in her mobile kitchen is Gianya (sous), Ticaria (carpenter), Sandu (artist), Cassamen (hostler), Bavolio, Little Ruzo, and Gray Manek (all musicians).
- Esa is an ex-partner
- An orphan named Pim asked for work a few weeks ago. She pretends to employ him as an excuse to keep an eye on him. She puts a few coins in his palm every night at the end of his “shift”. In truth, she is waiting for someone trust worthy to accompany him up to Kaer Maga.

Who are the men with big appetites?
- **Bolgar Grumm** (*demanding male dwarf merchant*) an his two sons Tuggus and Marlus.
- They are brining a heavily laden cart of Janderhoff black marble (285 stone). Bolgar intends to flip the marble for inflated grain, which he can then trade for (probably stolen) ponies. These beasts will amount to a small fortune back at the sky citadel. 
- Bolgar is upset with the Duskwarden and he will tell anyone willing to listen. He claims Warden Lok is corrupt and that she lied to prevent him and his sons from entering the city today. When he refused to pay a fee more than 3x what others are charged, she sent him and his sons to the back of the line. He intends to have a stern word with Warden Hammerfell when they reach the top.
- He attempted to bribe the warden to jump the line, in fact. He was already at the back.

## Morning

The runes are in no known language. Staring for more than a moment at the door discovers that the apparent simple construction plays tricks on the eyes—the gate’s edges subtly twist and warp, so that while all the edges seems straight and simple, anyone attempting to follow one with her eyes or fingers finds herself somehow at a different one, the outer edge becoming the inner one without seeming to twist, and so on.

Sunlight has crept halfway across the clearing before the door opens. Out file a small knot of uniformed figures, lightly armored and carrying weapons. The uniforms are brown and gray with a sigil on the right breast—a golden arch against a midnight blue background. These are the Duskwardens.

The first order of business is taking a headcount. The Duskwarden take inventory of the travelers and cargo by weight. Notably the cargo is *not* inspected. The wardens have a brief discussion before dividing everyone up into groups of twelve. 

The PCs are approached by this guy.

![[abra_lopati.jpg]]

Abra looks to be about 30 years old, with a thick brown beard and hard but handsome features. He greets the PCs affably and informs them that as a bunch that looks like they can pull their weight, they’re going in group 1 with. He then waves over several more people—whom he is less eager to welcome into his group—including Bolgar Grumm and his sons. Once the merchant has pulled his cart, Abra lays out the rules for the ascent.

Each PC is charged 2 gp to use the Halflight Path—the minimum allowable, Abra assures them—while the merchant with his cart is charged significantly more. Once they pass through the Twisted Door, there is to be no talking unless absolutely necessary, and absolute silence when Abra signals for it. Bolgar, stinging from the fee, looks inclined to object, but Abra empahsizes that while the Duskwarden attempt to keep the path safe, there are still things in the Undercity that they don’t want to risk metting, and that the whole reason they operate in small groups is to maintain a certain level of stealth, as well as to make it easier to fight in cramped quarter if necessary. At the last addendum, he looks significantly at the PCs.

After answering any questions the PCs have, he hands each one a small crystal on a thong—items he calls *halflight charms*. Once everyone is wearing one, he nods, and explains that if they get separated for any reason, the wearer can use a charm to call for help. He also explains that he will be collecting them at the end of their journey. So, don’t lose them.
