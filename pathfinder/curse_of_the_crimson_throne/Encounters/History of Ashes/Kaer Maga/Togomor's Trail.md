## The Truth
Togomor practices a type of hemotheurgy known as bloatmagic. While they are not exclusive to Kaer Maga, the City of Strangers is home to a sizable cabal of these sorcerers. Contrary to rumor and the moniker, bloatmages are not corpulent — practitioners learned early on that muscle carries more blood that fat. As a result, the truth is that most hemotheurges are extremely fit, often strikingly muscular, and many are physically larger than average—whether by natural or magical means.

Before he left Kaer Maga, to join Ileosa in her campaign, Togomor was conducting proxy research for the Korvosan queen here in The Hex. Ileosa charges Togomor with researching the effects of pregnancy on hemotheurgical power. Her reasoning being, pregnancy increases the amount of blood present in the mother’s body. The larger the foetus, the more total blood is stored. The question is whether the mother would be able to add this to her own _blood pool_. The secondary question, of course, if what adverse effect this might have on the foetus.

Togomor had fewer obstacles to conducting this research in Kaer Maga. For one, money can buy test subjects—the slave markets make that a simple matter. Another reason, is the lack of legal restriction on terminating pregnancies (or much else for that matter) in The Hex. Finally, hemotheurgy is openly practiced in Kaer Maga. While not illegal in Korvosa, that’s only because it hasn’t come up.

The program lasted the better part of a year. The results were quite promising. Yes, the foetus adds to the mother’s blood pool. Yes, there are adverse effects on the foetus. While Togomor’s research was not even close to the worst thing going on in Kaer Maga, he decided to keep it a secret from most of his fellow practitioners in the city. Uncovering the details of what happened will require a good bit of investigation by the PCs.

The basis for the Queen’s legislative agenda regarding reproductive rights in Korvosa is rooted in her own imminent need to be pregnant, terminate those pregnancies with some frequency, and be free of prosecution for such by the Arbiters—though few remain who are not either loyal or cowed from the Gray Maiden takeover of Longacre.

## The Trail
How do the PCs try to find out more about Togomor?
1. Find bloatmages in Kaer Maga and question them (Gather Information).
2. Find bloatmage critics in Kaer Maga and question them (Gather Information).
3. Do your own research on hemotheurgy (Investigate).

```pf2e-stats
# Question Bloatmages
---

==exploration== ==secret==

**Source** *Curse of the Crimson Throne Remastered*
**Skill** DC 25 Diplomacy (untrained)

---

Find bloatmages in Kaer Maga and question them about Togomor.
**Critical Success** As success but they’re a rival of Togomor and willingly give up what they know.
**Success** You find a bloatmage that knows where Togomor was working prior to his departure, but they want something in return for that lead.
**Failure** You find a bloatmage that knows where Togomor was working but they were involved and will not give up the information willingly.
**Critical Failure** The bloatmages who assisted Togomor don’t like you snooping around. They’re going to set you up and lead you into an ambush.
```
