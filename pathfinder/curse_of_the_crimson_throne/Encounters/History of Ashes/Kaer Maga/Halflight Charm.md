```pf2e-stats
# Halflight Charm
## Item 3

---
==uncommon== ==light== ==magical==

**Price** 55 gp
**Usage** worn; Bulk —

This amulet is a small crystal at the end of a simple leather thong. It glows brightly from within, shedding light equivalent to that of a torch.

---

Activate `[one-action]` (interact) **Frequency** Once per day

The amulet's bearer can clutch the object and call for help. The three nearest Duskwardens immediately learn your direction as a _locate_ spell, with a range of 1 mile. The charm pulses red for 1 hour, and any of those Duskwardens can sense the direction of your charm for the duration.
```
