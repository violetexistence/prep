# Kaer Maga
## Settlement 7
---
==city== ==criminal==

The Asylum Stone, City of Strangers, The Hex is a lawless refuge coiled inside a hollow ring of seamless Thassilonian stone, where every exile is welcome, every good has a price, and no one asks what you were before you climbed the Halflight Path.

**Government** Anarchy. Eleven districts, each policed by whoever holds it. A golem-crafting dynasty here, a mob of abolitionists there, a dark naga's protection racket down the road. Disputes are settled by reputation, retalliation, or the removeal of finger joints.

**Population** 8,000 (69% human, 6% halfling, 5% dwarf, 5% aiuvarin, 5% dromaar, 2.5% gnome, 1% elf, 15 orc, 1% troll, 5.5% other, plus nagas, centaurs, and the shambling mindless undead locals call the Twice-Born)

**Languages** Taldane (Common) is the universal trade language. Others vary by district. See [[Slang in Kaer Maga]].

**Religions** Abadar and Asmodeus foremost. In a city with no courts, the gods of contracts do brisk business. Also Calistria, Cayden Cailean (loud in the Bottoms), Nethys, Pharasma, Shelyn (largest shrine is at the Lyceum), Urgathoa, and the child-goddess of Ankar-Te. Sarenrae and Iomedae are tolerated nowhere but the Bottoms.

**Threats** Upwellings from the Undercity when Duskwarden seals fail; Necromancers in Ankar-Te shopping for vessels; The Flesh Block and the caravans it draws; Open sectarian war between the Brothers of the Seal in Oriat; Dependence on Korvosan grain; The blank patch on the southern Pillar of Dream, which the old-terms swear is growing.

**Anything Goes** Nothing is contraband. Undead labor, forbidden formulas, poisons, stolen goods, and people are traded openly. Items up to level 12 can be purchased normally.

**The Duskwardens · _Bis_** Subterranean rangers who seal the Undercity, patrol its upper levels, and guide the Halflight Path. They keep the **Map Room**, the most complete record of the Undercity in existence. **Leader** Rogard Hammerfell (grizzled male dwarven ranger). **Wants** the city safe and the Path open. **Offers** passage, records, credibility. **Avoids** familial or factional ties.

**Ardoc Family · _Bis_** Golem crafters bound by blood and marriage, somwhere between magistrates and mafia dons. They keep order in Bis by strict, just, brutal law -- sentences measured in "knuckles" carried out on the spot with chisels every brother wears. The Kiln is factory, fortress, courthouse. **Leader** Merriman Ardoc (wise male human wizard). **Wants** order and contracts honored. **Offers** weapons and constructs at scale. **Avoids** causes without terms.

**Arcanists' Circle · _Tarheel Promenade_** Wizards and sorcerers funding their individual projects with a coop run magic shop on the ground floor of their guildhouse, the Wheel Unbroken. **Leader** Suthevan Gyves (savvy male aiuvarin wizard) **Wants** to collaborate and share knowledge; **Offers** libraries, magic items, community; **Avoids** admitting members from divine traditions
