# Lissala's Whip
## Item 17

---
The jade-handled whip the statue holds can be removed with a little bit of work. It is a _+2 axiomatic whip_ but carries a potent curse. A creature that invests this item, makes a secret DC 38 Will save to resist the effects of an ancient curse.
The curse manifests as vague visions of menacing but indistinct shapes covered with runes lurking now and then at the edge of the accursed's vision. The accursed believes that Thassalonian writing holds some deeper threat to them and their loves ones. The curse can only be lifted by returning the whip to the statue's hand, or a successful counteract ability (rank 9).
**Critical Success** No effect
**Success** Stupefied 1
**Failure** Stupefied 1, frightened 1 while any Thassalonian writing is visible to the cursed creature
**Critical Failure** Stupefied 2, frightened 2 while any Thassalonian writing is visible to the cursed creature
