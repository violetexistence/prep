## Wandering Monster Prompts

Roll 1d20 on each list when the Wandering Monster table calls for that type, or pick whichever fits the moment. (Creature results use the zone tables instead.)

### Terrain

1. The ash crust here is thinner than it looks; one wrong step and a leg goes through to the hollow beneath.
2. A dry wash the map shows as passable is choked with a recent rockslide.
3. A stand of cinder-glass spires, razor-edged, forces a slow, careful detour.
4. The hex's one obvious trail dead-ends at a crumbled overhang, no way down.
5. A field of sinkholes, invisible until weight is on them, pocks the route.
6. Loose scree turns every uphill step into two steps back.
7. A ravine splits the hex in two, deeper than it looked from a distance.
8. The ground here rings hollow underfoot — a lava tube runs just below the surface.
9. Standing heat shimmer makes distances and drop-offs impossible to judge accurately.
10. A dry riverbed, easy walking, but the banks show signs of a recent flash flood.
11. Ash dunes have swallowed whatever trail used to run through here.
12. A field of obsidian shards, sharp enough to cut boots and hands alike.
13. The hex's only shade is a rock overhang already claimed by something's droppings and bones.
14. Ground tremors underfoot, faint but unmistakable, from something moving far below.
15. A collapsed cinder cone has left a crater that swallows the direct path.
16. Cracked earth here vents faint sulfurous steam from below.
17. A natural bridge of stone spans a gorge — narrow, and not obviously stable.
18. The trail forks around a field of petrified trees, eerie and disorienting.
19. Wind-carved hoodoos turn the hex into a maze even with a map in hand.
20. A stretch of black glass ground, brittle, cracks loudly with every step.

### Weather

1. An ash storm rolls in fast, cutting visibility to arm's length and burying whatever trail was underfoot.
2. The heat steps up a full category for the day, no warning, no relief.
3. A dust devil tears through camp, scattering anything not tied down.
4. Cold drops hard after sunset — a rare enough shift here to catch everyone unprepared.
5. Static charge builds in the air all afternoon; metal gear starts to hum.
6. A haboob wall, visible for miles, gives just enough warning to dig in before it hits.
7. Ash rain falls for hours, fine and grey, coating everything and everyone.
8. The wind reverses hard at midday, driving heat and grit back the way the party came.
9. A dry lightning storm crawls the horizon all night, striking without a drop of rain.
10. Heat lightning flickers over a distant cinder cone, closer than it looks.
11. An emberstorm — the omen kind, not just the weather kind — lights the horizon red.
12. Thick haze settles over the hex, muffling sound and cutting sightlines.
13. A sudden, brief downpour turns ash to slurry for an hour before the heat burns it off again.
14. Wind scours a fine grit into every seam of armor and pack alike.
15. The sky yellows before noon — a sandstorm building somewhere upwind.
16. A cold, dry wind off the mountains cuts the heat but brings its own misery.
17. Mirage-heat distorts the horizon all day, playing tricks on tired eyes.
18. Ashfall from a distant, unseen eruption drifts down like grey snow.
19. A still, breathless heat settles in — worse, somehow, than any wind.
20. Clouds gather and threaten all day, then break apart at dusk without a drop falling.

### Supplies

1. Weevils have gotten into the grain stores; salvage what's left or toss the lot.
2. A waterskin's seam has failed, and no one noticed until it was half-empty.
3. The day's heat has curdled or spoiled a portion of the rations outright.
4. Scavengers got into an unguarded pack overnight, and made off with the jerky.
5. What was marked as clean water turns out to carry a foul, silty taste — safe, but barely.
6. A pack animal has been favoring salted meat over its own feed, and now there's less of both.
7. Someone's personal stash gets discovered empty — eaten in secret, hunger winning over discipline.
8. The last of the fresh fruit has finally turned; it's compost now, not food.
9. A cracked waterskin has been slowly leaking for a day before anyone catches it.
10. Ants have found the sugar and honey stores; they're not giving them back easily.
11. What should be three days of rations turns out, on recount, to be closer to two.
12. Grit and ash have worked into the flour; it's edible, but unpleasant, and there's less usable than it looks.
13. A sudden downpour soaks the dried goods faster than anyone can cover them.
14. The last full waterskin turns out to have been drained already, quietly, by someone thirstier than they admitted.
15. Flies have found the fresh meat before it can be smoked or salted.
16. A rockslide, or a startled animal, sends one pack tumbling — with the coffee in it.
17. Someone miscounted at the last supply stop; there's a real gap that's only now obvious.
18. The salt ran out days ago, and preserved meat is starting to show it.
19. A skin of water was mistaken for a skin of oil and used for the fire — badly.
20. What's left of the bread has gone hard as stone; still edible, if teeth hold out.

### Equipment

1. A boot sole finally gives out, ash working its way in with every step from here on.
2. Heat has warped a blade just enough that it doesn't sit right in its sheath anymore.
3. A tent stake snapped setting up camp, and there's no easy replacement out here.
4. Rope, brittle from sun and ash, frays through at the worst possible moment to notice.
5. A waterskin's stopper has cracked, and it won't seal reliably anymore.
6. Grit has worked into a lock mechanism; the chest won't open without real effort now.
7. A bowstring, dried out by the heat, snaps mid-draw.
8. Buckles and clasps have corroded from sweat and ash — armor doesn't sit quite right.
9. A lantern's oil has thickened in the heat and won't light cleanly.
10. Someone's pack strap tears at the seam, dumping half its contents before anyone notices.
11. A whetstone's worn down to nothing; edges are getting duller by the day.
12. Fine ash has fouled a weapon's action; it needs real cleaning before it's reliable again.
13. A map case wasn't sealed properly, and the map inside took real damage.
14. Leather straps, dried stiff, crack rather than flex when it matters.
15. A cookpot's handle has worked loose and finally gives way over the fire.
16. Sand has worked its way into every joint of a suit of armor; it creaks now.
17. A spare weapon's edge has gone rusty despite the dry heat — sweat and neglect.
18. A holy symbol or focus item's fine chain has snapped; it needs proper repair.
19. Wind and grit have scoured the fletching off half a quiver's arrows.
20. A cartographer's kit ink has dried out in the heat, unusable until replenished.
