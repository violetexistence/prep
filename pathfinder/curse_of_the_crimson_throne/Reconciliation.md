## Threshold
**Composure 5**

The hunting party assembles in the yard of the Three Pardons, a posting inn a few miles north of Korvosa, on the same road you took to Harse. It's the kind of establishment that exists to service exactly this purpose—wealthy parties requiring a staging ground that isn't the city. The innkeeper has been awake since before dawn. The yard smells of horses and cold air and the woodsmoke form the kitchen where something substantial is being prepared for the return.

The morning is clear and hard. It's the first taste of non-plague ridden, non Korvosan air you've had in months. The sky is a pale iron blue, the ground frozen to a satisfying firmness underfoot, with breath fogging at every exhale. The bare tree-line to the north is dark against the sky. Good hunting country. Everyone knows it.

The yard is controlled chaos in the way that well-organized things look chaotic from the outside. Grooms moving horses. Servants distributing equipment. Hounds in a separate pen whose noise provides a constant undercurrent to everything. Perhaps thirty people assembling into something that will eventually look like a party, currently looking like a collection of individuals performing their own private preparations.

The Gray Maiden are already positioned. Four of them, in riding adaptation of their armor, moving with the particular economy of soldiers doing a job. They don't circulate. They don't make small talk. They have sight-lines and they're keeping them. You clock them before you consciously decide to.

You note their positioning without meaning to. Force of habit.

Commander Merrin is not among them.

---
The Baroness von Carstein arrived twenty minutes early because the Baroness von Carstein always arrives twenty minutes early to anything that matters and has done so her entire life. She is standing near the inn’s entrance in conversation with a man you don’t recognize—well dressed, north-Chelish cut to his coat, the comfortable posture of someone who knows he belongs wherever he stands. Your mother’s back is to the yard. She is fully occupied.

You are standing approximately eight feet to your mother’s left, holding your own reins because it gives your hands something to do, for the first time since you arrived without specific instruction.

You horse is good. The Baroness saw to that. A dark bay gelding with a sensible eye who has been standing patiently while your grip on his reins communicates things to him that your face does not communicate to anyone watching you (**Composure**).

You look exactly like what you’re supposed to look like. You mother’s choices… mostly: *What are they?*
