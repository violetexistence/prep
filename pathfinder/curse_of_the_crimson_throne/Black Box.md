Upon closer examination and in the proper light, you see there are symbols etched into the iron, over nearly the entire surface of box. Some appear religious while other are merely fantastic. On the top side of the box, there are three rings of nine distinct symbols, which you believe can be rotated by pressing down with your hand and twisting. The rings are each the same size and the same distance from the other two, forming a triangle with one ring centered at each point. The symbols are oriented in a way such that they only appear right-side up when brought the point on the circle closest to the other two rings (into the center). 

At the center of each ring is a 10th symbol.

You study the etching around the plates for patterns. A surge of delight rushes through you…you’ve found one.

Each circular plate bears **nine** small symbols set just inside its rim. The three plates are spaced evenly from one another, forming a neat triangle. At the center of each plate is a **tenth**, larger symbol, distinct from the nine around it.

Scanning the symbols and experimenting with the rotations, you notice another detail: each symbol depicts an object, and each object has only one “upright” orientation. In every case, that correct orientation is the one turned toward the center of the triangle formed by the three plates.

You turn the plates in an attempt to line of three specific symbols… and find that turning one also turns the other two! What’s more, each plate appears to engage mechanisms with different ratios… 

**Secret Thievery Check Rides** Quickly, you calculate the ratios of the gears engaged by each plate when depressed and rotated. After some backtrack arithmetic, you manage to get the plates in position for **knife**, **mask**, and **cloak**. Listening closely, you can tell that the catch inside the box remains closed. This does not appear to be the combination.
