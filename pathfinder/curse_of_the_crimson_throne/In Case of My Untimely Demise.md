If you are reading this, then I have failed at something I once considered unthinkable: being present when I am needed.

Do not waste breath on grief. Grief is for later.

I am writing because there is a burden I have carried too long to pretend it is mine alone, and because I refuse to let it die with me simply because my hands can no longer hold it.

You have seen the mask. You have seen what it does to a city when someone, somewhere, refuses to kneel.

That refusal is not a sword technique. It is not a clever footwork drill. It is not even bravery, not really. It is a strange species of stubborn love, I think. Out of love, someone dons the mask, meets the tyrant’s gaze, and replies, “No. Not comfortable. Not unchallenged. Not while I still draw breath.”

Up to this moment, I cannot recall how long now, I have been that someone.

And now, if our city is to have him when she most needs him, *you* must be.

You will be tempted to protest. You will list the reasons you should not. You will try to hand this to someone you think more suitable. Someone cleaner, nobler, louder, stronger, straighter-backed.

I have considered them all. And I have chosen you anyway.

Because of all the unlikely heroes I have encountered, you possess two things rarer than talent: the cleverness to outwit evil when it cannot be overpowered, and a heart that refuses to quit especially when the tyrant is winning.

Since you will appreciate honesty, I will confess one selfish hope besides: I pray this mantle falls on someone with just enough vanity to keep the People’s Champion looking _good._ A symbol must be sharp. A rumor must be irresistible.

If you accept, understand what you are accepting.

You do not become a tyrant’s assassin.
You do not become an executioner.
You do not become a martyr unless there is no other choice.

You become a mask.

You will strike where corruption expects silence.
You will lift those the city pretends not to see.
You will keep your name out of their mouths, because names are handles, and evil has too many hands that like to pull.

You will do all of this YOUR OWN WAY. The mask is YOU now. It becomes you in time.

If you ever feel your purpose curdling into cruelty, if you begin to crave the sting more than the change it buys, take off the mask and walk away. No one is permitted to become what they hate simply because they were angry first. Least of all you.

With respect,
With affection,

He exhales, the memory tightening his jaw.

_But lately, even before the quarantine, he was spending more and more time on the island. In my estimation, he became obsessed with an investigation. His gaze drifts, caught on a last fond memory of the Maestro.

_When they burned the bridges, I was trapped on this side. I didn’t know what to do. My home—my house—is in South Shore. A sad tenderness creeps into his voice.
