Here are eleven who stayed.

---

**1. Serafine Aldric** — _Human, she/her, age 19_ Her family is in Korvosa. They begged her to come home. She refused, and it fractured something between them that hasn't healed yet. She stayed because leaving felt like admitting the temple was just a building, and she cannot afford to believe that. She is rigorous, occasionally self-righteous, and privately terrified that she stayed for pride rather than faith. The plague burned a lot of her certainty away and she is rebuilding it brick by brick, not always in the same shape.

---

**2. Tomás Vierren** — _Human, he/him, age 24_ Came to the temple late, after a previous life he doesn't discuss in detail. Old enough to have seen hard things before the plague. He stayed because the work during the crisis was the first time in years he felt like his hands were doing something that mattered. He is calm in emergencies, useful, not particularly pious in the singing-and-radiance sense. Beryndr'n valued him for that. He is not sure how he feels about Ahalya yet.

---

**3. Priya Sunweather** — _Human, she/her, age 16_ An orphan the temple took in when she was eight. She has nowhere else to go in the literal, logistical sense. But she also stayed because the temple is the only family she has, and she would rather die here than survive somewhere that means nothing to her. She is young enough that the plague marked her in ways she hasn't fully processed. She latched onto Orren during the worst of it and he let her, quietly, without making it a whole thing.

---

**4. Davin Kels** — _Human, he/him, age 21_ His family insisted he come home. He wrote back that he would, and then didn't, and has been avoiding their letters since. He is not sure why he stayed — he doesn't have a clean answer for himself. He thinks it might be a girl in the east ward who died in the third week and whose name he can't stop thinking about. He is working that out through staying. He is a little lost. He is genuinely kind.

---

**5. Zivara** — _Gnome, she/her, apparent age indeterminate, likely 40s in gnome years_ She is older than she looks, which is always true of gnomes, and she has survived worse than this, which she does not say aloud because it would be unkind. She stayed because gnomes who have found a place they feel genuinely _warm_ do not leave it lightly. The temple of Sarenrae is the warmest place she has ever been. She was invaluable during the plague — gnome constitution, relentless energy, a completely unsentimental approach to the practical problems of mass death. She made people laugh at moments when that seemed impossible. She misses Beryndr'n in a way that is almost proprietary, like losing someone who finally got her.

---

**6. Brother Aldous Fenwick** — _Human, he/him, age 67_ He is not precisely a novice in the traditional sense — he came to Sarenrae late in life, after his wife died, as a way of finishing out his years with purpose. He deferred his formal training twice because of his knees. He stayed during the plague because at sixty-seven, dying of a plague in a temple doing good work strikes him as a perfectly acceptable way to go. He survived, which he regards with mild surprise. He is slow, thorough, occasionally oblivious to subtext, and beloved by everyone. He calls Orren "young man" without irony.

---

**7. Nessa Orvaine** — _Half-orc, she/her, age 22_ She grew up in Korvosa's lower city, which means she stayed because this was the safest place available to her and also because she has never once in her life had the luxury of running toward safety when things got hard. She is direct, has a low tolerance for institutional hypocrisy, and is one of the few novices who will say plainly what she thinks. She was invaluable in the plague wards because patients from the lower city trusted her. She has opinions about the Church's relationship to power that she is learning, slowly, to express in ways that don't get her in trouble.

---

**8. Emric Thal** — _Human, he/him, age 17_ He stayed because his older brother, also a novice, died in the fourth week of the plague and leaving felt like abandoning him. The logic doesn't hold up under scrutiny — his brother is gone, not here — but grief doesn't always hold up under scrutiny. He is quiet, good with his hands, and has taken on tasks that keep him moving so he doesn't have to sit still. He will need, at some point, to sit still.

---

**9. Thessaly Vorne** — _Human, she/her, age 28_ She is a former midwife who came to the temple specifically because she wanted formal religious training to go alongside her medical skills. She stayed during the plague because what was happening in the wards was an extension of everything she already knew how to do: sit with people in the worst moments of their bodies and not look away. She is the most technically skilled of the novices in healing work. She and Beryndr'n had a complicated relationship — Thessaly occasionally pushed back in ways that were inconvenient, and Beryndr'n occasionally pushed back harder. They respected each other. Thessaly is one of the people Ahalya will need to win over.

---

**10. Calder Ashvoss** — _Tiefling, he/him, age 23_ He stayed because leaving would have confirmed something he has spent his whole life refusing to confirm — that he doesn't belong, that when it matters, he won't be there. His infernal heritage is visible and Korvosa is not always a kind city. The temple was one of the few institutions that took him at his word. During the plague he worked the night shifts, partly from insomnia, partly because the patients who were delirious or frightened at three in the morning were sometimes easier to comfort when the person sitting with them had strange eyes and already knew something about being afraid of what you are. He has never said that aloud. He might say it to Orren someday.

---

**11. Sable** — _Human, they/them, age 15 — the new aspirant_ They arrived at the temple gates eleven days ago, during the strange quiet after the plague ended. They were filthy, alone, and carrying a small Sarenrae medallion that was clearly not theirs — too fine, too old, found or gifted or perhaps taken from someone who no longer needed it. They asked to speak to the Dawnmother. They were told the Dawnmother had recently died. They asked to speak to whoever was in charge now. They were brought to Ahalya.

They have not explained where they came from or why now, and they haven't been pressed yet. They simply said they wanted to learn to be of use in Sarenrae's name, and their face when they said it was so nakedly sincere that Ahalya said yes before she'd thought it through.

They are present at the funeral because they have been at the temple for eleven days and the temple is already the whole of their world. They don't know who Beryndr'n was yet. They are watching everyone else to learn how to grieve here.
