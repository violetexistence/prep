```pf2e-stats
# Brutal Strike `[free-action]`
---
==harrow==

Gain a +5 circumstance bonus to damage on all your melee Strikes for 1 minute. Alternatively, ignore your target's hardness until the end of your turn.
```
```pf2e-stats
# Mighty Thews `[free-action]` or `[two-actions]`
---
==harrow==

Gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Acrobatics and Athletics for 10 minutes. If you spend `[two-actions]`, you also grow to size Large. Your equipment grows with you but returns to natural size if removed. While large, you are clumsy 1, your reach increases by 5 feet (or 10 feet if you started out Tiny), and you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to damage rolls on melee Strikes. If you grow to size Large, Mighty Thews gains the ==polymorph== trait.
```
