## KORVOSAN CARRIER
*ALL THE CITY, FIT TO PRINT - PRICE: 1 COPPER PINCH - JEGGARE ROW, NORTH POINT*

TODAY’S WEATHER: Cold, bright, and biting. Expect river-fog at dusk.
COUNTDOWN: 5 days to Coronation, 9 bells 21 Kuthona

## PLAGUE CONQUERED!
By decree from our Radiant Queen, the quaratine of Old Korvosa will soon be lifted, following confirmation that new Blood Veil cases have ceased and remaining wards are convalescing. Ferry traffic and the bridges will be restored as soon as possible, with inspectors remaining stationed at all crossings.

Yet the city’s relief is tempered by a continued security posture: Old Korvosa will remain under martial law, “to facilitate ongoing investigations into foreign conspirators and agents suspected to have aided the plague and the attempted destabilization of the realm.” Civil offices in Old Korvosa are instructed to provide records, manifests, and cooperation to the Gray Maidens and the Korvosan Guard, and otherwise not impeded investigative operations.

A public statement commended the Sister Merrin and Sister Kordaitra of the Gray Maiden for, “holding the line when strain might have broken lesser insititutions,” and for supporting the Korvosan Guard through unprecendeted hardship. The Crown likewise thanked the Arbiters, the Temple of Sarenrae, and numerous other groups who lent their aid in the city’s time of great need. Specifically, the Oracle of Sarenrae was named for her role in exposing the betrayer Reiner Devaulus and disrupting what officials described as a “Nidalese-backed plot to unseat lawful rule in Korvosa.”

Citizens are urged to report suspicious foreigners—particularly those unable to name a sponsor or fixes residence—directly to the Gray Maiden chapter house at Longacre.

## “THE WORK IS NOT DONE”
GRAY MAIDEN TO EXPAND NIGHT PATROLS IN OLD KORVOSA

Sister Sabina Merrin stood with Field Marshal Kroft beneath the black pennants of Citadel Volshyenek, addressing a small crowd of concerned business owners, confirmed that Gray Maiden patrols will intensify within Old Korvosa over the next tenday. “Quarantine ends,” she stated. “Weakness does not.”

When pressed on reports of remaining Nidalese agents, Merrin offered only: “There are always conspirators who believe darkness makes them invisible. We will educate them.”

Under the martial law order, Gray Maiden may conduct searches, question travelers, and request access to public and private houses alike. The Korvosan Guard will continue joint patrols, with additional support from Sable Company patrols on the Jeggare.

## CORONATION ANNOUNCED
PUBLIC CEREMONY IN DOMINA SQUARE; ENTRY INSTRUCTION INCLUDED

The coronation ceremony is confirmed for 21 Kuthona, at nine bells, in Domina Square. The Crown describes the event as public with order attendance expected, and asks citizens to “arrive early, be patient with security checks, and show Korvosa’s best face to visiting dignitaries.”

A map of entry lanes and viewing areas will be posted at the square by 19 Kothona. The Lords Magistrate advises families to keep children close and leave pack animals and carts outside the inner cordon.

## A CITY THAT ENDURES MUST BE A CITY THAT OBEYS

This city has learned a cruel arithmetic: when panic multiplies, order must divide it. Quarantine was not mercy. It was necessity. Martial law is not comfort. It is the price of waking tomorrow with our throats uncut and our granaries unburned.

Some will ask why Old Korvosa must remain under the iron hand when the fever has been beaten back. We answer: because enemies do not declare themselves defeated merely because we are tired.

Let those who whisper “tyranny” remember the empty beds, the shuttered shops, the hush of a street that used to laugh. Remember the Guard, who stood watch when their own families coughed behind closed doors. Remember the Maidens, who took the weight of our anger and did not step aside.

We will celebrate the lifting of quarantine. We will celebrate the solstice. But let celebration not make up sloppy. A city that endures must also at times be a city that obeys.

## THE QUIETER LIGHTS OF THE SEASON
Candlemark, Crystalhue, Stardust—The Prayers We Don’t Print Enough

Korvosa loves a spectacle. We love the crash of drums and the bright flare of a bonfire, and we are never shy about turning a holy day into a street fair if it means a sweetcake can be sold.

In the coming days we will hear much the Days of Wrath (Dies Irae)—and yes, the city’s memory has earned the right to be angry at sickness, at hunger, at those who profited from both. The games will be loud. The banner will snap. The cheers will be honest.

But quieter lights are gathering too.

Candlemark reminds us that a single flame can outlast a long night. Crytalhue is the moment we remember beauty is frivolous—it is a testament to our survival. The Ritual of Stardust is the small audacity of looking up and believing the sky has not forgotten us.

And beneath all that bustle, the faithful of Sarenrae offering food without fanfare; the devotees of Shelyn tying ribbons on repaired instruments; the stargazers of Desna teaching children the names of constellations.

If you see a quiet ceremony this week, don’t mock it for being small. Korvosa is alive, and that is made of thousands of small things.

## KEY LOCK KILLER CAPTURED?

Watch sources claim arrest in Veldraine; Volshyenek offers not confirmation.

Reports spread through Midland at midmorning like spilled lamp oil: the Key Lock Killer—the figure blamed for a string of quiet, methodical murder in shuttered buildings across Korvosa—has been taken alive.

If true, it would be the first clean ending to a story that has left too many doors bolted from the inside and too many families afraid to sleep behind a latch.

According to two separate watch sources who spoke on condition of anonmyity, a suspect was seized on the coast road north of Veldraine and handed to authorities at the naval base to be transferred to the dungeons beneath Longacre.

The Carrier has been unable to verify any details or that the sources are correct through official channels.

The Gray Maiden chapter house refused to comment, as did inspectors working for the Citadel beyond, “Investigations are ongoing. Do not interfere.” A clerk at the Ledger confirmed only that a “detainee of interest” was transferred under guard shortly before noon yesterday; no name was provided and no charge was listed on the public docket as of printing.

WHAT WE KNOW—AND WHAT WE DON’T

The Key Lock Killer story has never had the clean clarity of street-corner knife work. The killings were reported sporadically across the last ten years, often discovered days later in building already emptied by thieves and scavengers. In several cases, neighbors swore the victims had barred their doors and chained them from within. Yet the bodies were found inside, the lock intact.

Whether the murders were the work of one killer, a copycat, or something more organized has remained a matter of fierce debate between ward captains and every tavern hall in Korvosa.

Today’s rumor offers a satisfying shape: one culprit, one set of hands, one ring of keys. That shape may be true. It may also be exactly what frightened people want most.

## DIES IRAE GAMES: SLATE SET
REGIONAL QUALIFIERS FINISHED; KORVOSA FAVORED—BUT WHO WEARS THE CITY’S LAURELS?

The bells haven’t even finished arguing with the wind and already the bookies are sharpening their quills: the Dies Irae matches that “officially” begin the season of wrath are upon us, and the Inner Sea is sending its best to be bruised into legend.

This year’s slate is built like a gauntlet—regional qualifiers first, then the branded “Hateful 8” brackets in the solstice week, with the grand bouts timed to keep crowds loud, coin moving, and grudges conveniently public.

Korvosa has survived the qualifiers. That’s not surprising. What is this year is who carried the city’s title into the finals.

The Carrier has heard the argument in dockside taverns, salon parlors, and the echoing corridors of training halls. The city isn’t split so much as it is spoiled for choice.

And now we can report something that will turn those arguments into shouting.

CONFIRMED: IZZIE VON CARSTEIN ENTERS THE FIELD

Yes, *that* Izzie von Carstein—daughter of your own South Shore’s Baroness, newly inducted Gray Maiden sister—entered the lists and quietly steamrolled her opponents through the qualifiers. Yesterday, her name appeared on the official ledger, at the top above others expected to hold that honor such as Merrin or Ornelos.

The Maidens, as ever, offered no theatrical statement. “She’s disciplined. She’s earned her place among us and in this tournament.” A tone like that can mean admiration or warning, depending on who you are.

What does this mean? The only conclusion forgone at this point is who—the outcome is yet to be seen. Given her clean sweep in the regionals, the line is certain to continue shifting in the next few days.
