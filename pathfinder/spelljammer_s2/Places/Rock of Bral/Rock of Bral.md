**In Need of Repairs** Revelation is armed both port and starboard with 3-gun batteries and a ballista mounted on the focsle. However, there are currently no crew to operate these weapons and they are in need of repair. In their current state, they must check for misfire each time they are fired.

![[Sovyrian Corvette]]

![[Skyfire Cannon]]

*Revelation* itself is also currently down 40 HP from the job on Eox.

PCs can attempt to make repairs. If they do not have a [repair toolkit](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2747), Linetta shares that she does remember there were tools aboard at one time, but has not seen them lately. In fact, they are still aboard and they fell behind the stove in the galley after the ship’s former cook used them to repair the chimney. These grant a +1 item bonus to repair checks.

Without a proper shipyard, each attempt to repair the ship itself requires a full day.
