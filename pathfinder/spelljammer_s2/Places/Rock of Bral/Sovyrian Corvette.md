# Sovyrian Corvette
## Vehicle 12
---

==Uncommon== ==Gargantuan==

**Source** Harley's Angels S2

**Price** 7,500 gp

---

This triple-masted warship is crafted from sturdy duskwood brought up to the shipyards on Elindrae. Despite its modest firepower relative to size, smugglers and privateers favor this design for its ability to escape detection.

---

**Space** 90 feet long, 20 feet wide, 20 feet high

**Crew** 1 pilot, 20 crew; **Passengers** 20

**Piloting Check** Sailing Lore (DC 28), Diplomacy (DC 31), or Intimidation (DC 31)

---

**AC** 28; **Fort** +22

**Hardness** 20, **HP** 200 (BT 100); **Immunities** obeject immunities; **Weaknesses** 15 fire until broken

---

**Speed** swim 40 feet (wind, spelljamming)

**Collision** 8d10 (DC 30)

**Stealthy** Due to its ebony coloration, a sovyrian corvette is difficult to detect under certain conditions. It is concealed in dim light.

**Weapon Mounts** This vehicle has 2 weapon mounts, one on each side of the vehicle. At purchase, these weapon mounts are initially empty. Installing or uninstalling a weapon into a mount requires 1 day, a successful DC 28 Crafting check, and an appropriate weapon. The weapon mounts can be equipped with Huge or smaller ranged siege weapons.
