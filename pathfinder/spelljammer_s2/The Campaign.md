# The New Hotness
We return to the original S1 pitch: Episodic heist tv show. S1 kinda sorta started out that way but very much shifted into a more sandbox style game with a heavy meta plot that spent a long time on one world. We want more spelljamming, more world hopping, more heists.

How can we set this up and still feel like there is an overall story to tell?

Idea 1: Reset at the start of every session with a wrap up from the previous episode. Harley gives a new mission briefing. Sometimes it builds on the previous one, sometimes not. Even when it does, there is a progression—time passed and the situation has changed. Actions taken in the each mission do effect the outcome and this can be used to shape the evolving narrative, but the exit criteria are fixed. No “to be continued…” episodes, no two-parters allowed.

# The Old and Busted Concept

## The Campaign

When a dying knight swears the player characters to keep her oath, they get swept up in a quest to change the fate of an Aeldari Craftworld.

## Meaning

**What are good and evil?** The characters in this adventure will come to know the survivors of a once great empire, toppled by hedonistic excess and hunted by demons. These survivors have come to see their fate as a consequence not of supernatural forces beyond their control. Rather, they understand that each one of them carries within themselves light and dark. While demons do exist, they are merely manifestations of their own sins.

**But why are these beings so cursed?**

**Where did they acquire this terrible ability to manifest their own doom in such an unforgiving way?**

**Is there hope they might be redeemed?**

In our world, there is no proof of a supernatural force of good or evil. We are left to answer these types of questions ourselves. But in the Spelljammer inverse, in D&D, there are literal gods, magic is real and the characters can fight actual demons. The alignment system indicates that creatures are either good, neutral or evil. This means that characters in a story set in this universe can feel divorced from responsibility since these are forces of nature, not just the characteristics of people. Perhaps…

The power of the gods even in the Spelljammer universe is quite limited. They are powerful, yes. **But how powerful are they really?** Certainly a CR 30 god is a force to be reckoned with, but a party of 20th level characters can defeat one.

**What if the “gods” and all the supernatural forces of good and evil are themselves bundles of ordinary needs?**

This adventure plunges our characters into the midst of these questions. They will have the opportunity to help a civilization seek forgiveness and move on from their past, while the characters’ own backstories are explored.

## Summary

Tens of thousands of years in the past, as the story goes, the Aeldari built their civilization among the stars. They were able to traverse the full breadth of worlds using their unique paths through the Astral Sea. Owing to their affinity to arcane magic, they shaped entire planets, transforming them from barren rocks to verdant habitats. Their technology is still unrivaled by any civilization since. At the peak of their splendor, they feasted on pleasure and pain in such measure that their collective ecstasy gave birth to a god, one with an insatiable thirst. This is Slaanesh.

All that the Aeldari created was quickly devoured. All except for a handful of colonies, preserved on gargantuan colony ships, called Craftworlds, which now roam the universe hiding from She Who Thirsts.

The survivors abjure the temptations of their inner natures, and adhere to strict codes of conduct, for fear that their desires will attract the attention of their unholy creation. They are scattered but each one in their way seeks to repair their lost civilization, hoping to find a path through the tangled skein of fate to escape this punishment and their own cursed passions.

## Plot Connection

The events in Season 1 connect with Season 2.

Long before the events on Ardhi, Queen Mat’s revolution, or the legend of Ser William Scrumdiddly, the Titans created the Maisha system. The order of that world was created by a primordial blueprint, a pattern, something coaxing order from the storm of chaos.

**Who were the Titans?**

**Where did they come from?**

Harley is a **Watcher**, an agent of the **Assimon Covenant**. All the PCs were her unwitting acolytes.

Algalon is a rival Watcher, another agent who believes the whole Maisha system needs re-origination.

**Q: What is the Aasimon Covenant?**

A: An agreement between the Powers and Vassal worlds throughout the multiverse to combat the influence of Void Lords. Vassal Worlds must send a tithe every seven years and in return the celestials take an oath to appear when summoned to defend them from evil.

**Q: What are the Powers?**

A: Aasimari divine souls who are descended from a being known as “The Maker”. Each one is referred to as a Power and is responsible for watching a different part of the universe.

**Q: What kind of tithe is required?**

A: There are different levels of tithe pledged by different worlds. The most common tithe is in the form of “blood”. Every seven years, children must be sent to the Powers. The children sent must demonstrate incredible resistance to mortal temptations or exhibit extraordinary abilities. They are all gifted in some way. The tithe is always symbolic. A gesture of submission and devotion to the light.

**Q: What happens to children who are tithed?**

A: They are transformed into vessels of the light. Some are also trained to serve in the Maker’s Angels, paladins who answer the prayers of Vassal Worlds and fight evil.

**Q: Who are the Watchers?**

A: These are creatures working for the Powers who investigate corruption throughout the multiverse. They have broad authority to root out the influence of the Void Lords wherever they find it. In circumstances where a world is entirely lost to darkness, Watchers may issue an order for “planetary re-origination”, a process that summons the Powers themselves to destroy an entire world, wiping it from existence, and returning its soul to the light.
