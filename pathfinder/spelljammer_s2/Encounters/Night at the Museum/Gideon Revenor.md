**Thoughtform Summoning** Whenever you summon a creature, you can choose to instead create a psychic construct of that creature. This grants it resistance to physical damage equal to half its level, weakness 5 to force and mental damage, and the ability to pass through enemies' spaces without needing to Tumble Through (though it can't end its turn in an occupied space).
