# Night at the Museum

![[corentyn_museum_of_history.jpg]]

- (Bond-style cold open) Corentyn / Night / Outside a museum / Cosmopolitan fantasy town
- On the steps of a museum, a ram batters against iron gates, violent thuds punctuating a public speech.
- The speaker—a uniformed man wearing the dottari (local constabulary) sigil—bellows, “I declare the Museum of History to be in possession of seditious material. It is my duty to seize and destroy all offending objects, and to subdue all who obstruct me.” At the side of the dottari, another man dressed in black—no sigil whatsoever.
- “Where’s your warrant?” asks the curator as he retreats from the gaps in the bars toward the museum. He gives one of the tipped benches a final shove against the barricade. The gates rattle with the push.
- “You’ll find that I need no warrant, Zenfiro.” With a snap, the man in black disappears. A panicked shout from the other side of the gates betrays his new location—on the steps of the museum, between the curator and safety.
- A company of armed troops forms a semi-circle to protect the dottari from an angry crowd forming on the street.

![[Crowncrusher Riot]]

- A few of the troops are using a portable ram to break down the barricade.
- Gideon Ravenor is the man in black, a covert operative is here to seize or destroy sensitive material on Corentyn and interrogate those in possession of such material, like Zenfiro.

![[Gideon Revenor]]

![[Young Underworld Dragon]]

- The Angels are here to extract an asset, codename: Silhouette. If they fail, she will be arrested attempting to rescue Zenfiro.
- Silhouette is sneaking (DC 32 Seek) up on the museum steps to rescue Zenfiro. This is part of a coordinated operation by several rebel groups, both local and global, to overthrow Corentyn’s government.

**Creatures:** The excruciator divisions ram against the museum’s gates, obeying their orders from the local hegemon to support Sabbathiel. The first step of their plan is to prevent external forces from interrupting the raid. Their next objectives are to open the gate, provide backup to Ravenor, and loot the museum.
