# Safehouse

[[Linetta Seacarver|Linetta]] chose this building for its lack of anything remarkable. It has no windows and is claimed by no merchant factor on Corentyn of note, being one of a hundred that used to operate as storage for the FSMC. Its doors are always locked and Linetta is careful to never enter in broad daylight where anyone can observe her activity. The warehouse's actual use is exclusively for secret meetings between underworld agents from both on and off world. Linetta is one of only three people with a key to the safehouse.

Inside there are rotting crates, old barrels, and moldering sacks of a dozen lost cargo shipments arranged to appear uninteresting and burdensome. In the center of the maze of cargo is a small discreet meeting area with a single table with a lantern and no chairs. When the PCs arrive, Linetta is here with the lantern lit, observing their progress via her animal companion, Grackle and a cadre of local muscle.

A dashing corsair captain sporting a feathered hat, leather baldric, and surrounded by a half dozen rough looking enforcers, smiles as you come into the light.

"You made it. Incredibly clean work back there with the dottari. Seeing you give those bootlickers the laugh warms my heart… right—no sense delaying our departure, eh? Let's shove off…"

"Not so fast, Seacarver. Where's our gold?" interjects one of her enforcers.

The corsair captain rolls her eyes casually, "Really, Traevus. You made plenty, already. Now is not the time to get greedy."

Traevus growls and steps menacingly toward her, leveling a pistol waist height. "You're fixing to peel after we bled and good? Pay us what's owed… or feed the wyrm."

Seacarver gestures with open hands, "Hey, hey, no need to get nasty. If you really feel that way, I'm sure we can work something out. This is me—I'm good for it…"

The sound of the hammer being cocked back is the only reply—along with an I dare you sneer. The corsair captain shifts her gaze to you and raises an eyebrow.

Linetta promised to pay Traevus and his crew 600 gp for their help breaking into the decommissioned Castle Belverio and steal siege engine parts. The heist was successful and the parts are now in Revelation's cargo deck, hidden under a load of mule feed. Payment for the crew's services has not yet been rendered—mostly because she does not have the funds to do so, but also because she's confident the PCs will be motivated to make this problem go away.

**Negotiate** DC 29 Diplomacy to convince the Traevus's crew that making a deal is better than risking a fight and getting even less for their trouble.

**Critical Success** As success, but they will take half the agreed price.

**Success** Traevus agrees to give Seacarver one week to pay up. Until then, they're coming along to make sure no one tries to skip out.

**Failure** As success, but they want 100 gp now.

**Critical Failure** Pay in full now or they attack.

**Development** Any fight that breaks out in the safehouse lasting brings an [excruciator division](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=4823) led by an [accuser agent](https://2e.aonprd.com/NPCs.aspx?ID=3565) at the beginning of round 3.
