Drukhari assault skimmer used as a troop transport for Kabal raids into wildspace.

**Crew** 1 pilot, 1 crew, 8 passengers

**Piloting Check** Sailing Lore (DC 28), Diplomacy (DC 31), or Intimidation (DC 31)

**Swept Fairings** The DCs of Acrobatics and Athletics checks made to disembark a moving Raider are reduced -5.

---

**AC** 28; **Fort** +19

**Hardness** 15, **HP** 170 (BT 85); **Immunities** object immunities

**Explodes** Raiders reduced to 0 hp must succeed on a DC 5 flat check or the helm detonates. All creatures within 60 feet take 8d10 force damage (DC 30 basic Reflex).

---

**Speed** fly 40 feet (hover)

**Collision** 8d10 (DC 30)

**Eviscerating Fly-by** Creatures that fail their saving throw to avoid being Run Over also take 1d10 persistent bleed damage (2d10 on a critical failure).

---

**Weapon Mounts** This vehicle has 1 prow mounted weapon. At purchase, this weapon mount is initially empty. Installing or uninstalling a weapon into a mount requires 1 day, a successful DC 28 Crafting check, and an appropriate weapon. The weapon mount can be equipped with a Large or smaller ranged siege weapon.
