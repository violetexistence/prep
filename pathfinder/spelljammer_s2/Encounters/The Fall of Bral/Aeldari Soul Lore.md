# Aeldari Soul Lore

**Before the Fall**

Aeldari were never physically immortal in the traditional fantasy elf sense. Instead they possessed a natural cycle of rebirth — death was a transition rather than an ending. The soul was caught, preserved, and reincarnated in a new body, retaining memory and continuity of self across lifetimes. This meant Aeldari civilization was built on the accumulated personal experience of individuals who had lived and died and lived again across millennia. Their philosophers, warriors, artisans and leaders weren't just old — they were *multiply-lived*.

**The Fall**

The birth of She Who Thirsts didn't just destroy the Aeldari empire politically and militarily. It shattered the metaphysical infrastructure that made the rebirth cycle possible. Slaanesh emerged from the psychic excess of Aeldari civilization and in doing so became a predator specifically attuned to Aeldari souls. Death is no longer a transition — an unprotected Aeldari soul at the moment of death is immediately vulnerable to consumption.

The Fall didn't make Aeldari mortal. It put a hungry mouth at the headwaters of the river of life.

**Three Responses to the Break**

**Craftworld Aeldari — Soul Stones**

A partial technological and psychic workaround. Each Craftworld Aeldari carries a soul stone — grown or shaped specifically for the individual and attuned to their psychic signature over time. At the moment of death the stone catches and preserves the soul, protecting it from She Who Thirsts. Full rebirth in a new body is no longer reliably possible, but the soul is protected and can potentially commune with the living. Craftworld Infinity Circuits are vast repositories of preserved ancestor souls — not immortality, more like honored stasis. The soul stone is deeply personal and culturally significant — not a commodity but a birthright.

**Commorragh Aeldari — Haemonculi Tech**

The dark mirror solution. If the soul cannot be protected spiritually, continuity of self is maintained through flesh instead. Haemonculi are master flesh-crafters who have developed biological resurrection — backup bodies, consciousness transfer, physical cheating of death. It requires significant resources to maintain and those resources ultimately flow from the soul trade — causing sufficient suffering in others generates psychic energy that temporarily satiates She Who Thirsts enough to leave the practitioner alone. It is immortality of a kind but it is hungry immortality. A Commorragh Aeldari who stops feeding the machine begins to physically decay and fade.

**Corsairs and Outcasts — Largely Unprotected**

The most honest relationship with what was lost. Corsairs know they are mortal in a way their ancestors were not. Some carry soul stones acquired through Craftworld origin or extraordinary circumstance. Many don't. They live closer to the emotional edge, more joyful and more vulnerable in equal measure. Over time the absence of psychic protection creates a natural drift toward Commorragh and its solutions — not always through conscious choice but through accumulated existential pressure.

**Soul Stones — Significance and Acquisition**

A soul stone is not a purchasable commodity. It is grown or shaped specifically for an individual Aeldari and attuned to their psychic signature over time. Possessing one implies either Craftworld origin — born into that culture and given a stone as birthright — or an extraordinary relationship with a Craftworld significant enough that one was provided. A Corsair carrying a soul stone is therefore notable. It suggests someone who despite rejecting Craftworld culture retained enough connection or foresight to make provisions for their own continuity.

**Crow's Father**

A Corsair who carried a soul stone built into his armor. This marks him as either Craftworld-born originally or someone who earned or acquired a stone through significant means. Either way it indicates a man who wanted to persist — who took his own continuity seriously despite the Corsair lifestyle. His soul gem survived the pocket dimension collapse and was recovered by Yvraine, who had history with him from Commorragh, and delivered via Harley to the Veil of Isha where it currently resides in safety.

**Crow**

Half-Aeldari, half-human. Has no soul stone — she didn't know what she was and nobody provided her with one. Her Aeldari psychic sensitivity is awakening through a condition called Shai'eth-tor, the soul-sickness that manifests when Aeldari psychic nature expresses itself without the cultural framework or discipline to manage it. Her compulsive sensation-seeking and intense emotional responses to injustice are early symptoms already present in her characterization before the reveal.

As an unprotected awakening Aeldari soul operating in the Astral Sea where Commorragh has reach, Crow is existentially vulnerable in ways she doesn't yet understand. Vect's people would recognize what she is immediately.
