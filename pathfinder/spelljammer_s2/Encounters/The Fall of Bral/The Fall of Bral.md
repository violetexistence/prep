# The Fall of Bral

- As cover for the cleanup op to erase any trace of Harley's mission, Vect orders a corsair fleet to raid Bral. It is a devastating attack. Omg space elves are dicks.
- Maybe a choice for the players… Do we stick around and try to help save Bral? Or, do we get the hell out of here and escape with our lives?
- Should probably throw in some compelling NPCs to make the decision at least interesting.
- We get to test out a bunch of systems, including small scale combat, skirmishes, and the cinematic ship combat mechanics.
- Foreshadow the soul trade—the elves aren't killing everyone. Instead many are being taken captive.
- Introduce a sexy villain NPC leading troops during the raid that you get to fight for real later on.
- Make most things level 8 and the encounters moderate difficulty. The win conditions aren't about killing the enemies. The win conditions are: break through before reinforcements arrive, keep inhabitants from being taken as prisoners/slaves, acquire vehicles

## Children of Thorns

**Exile's Hunger** When a Child of Thorns grabs, restrains, or reduces a creature to 0 HP, they gain 10 temporary HP.

### Drukhari

![[drukhari_warrior.png]]

**Power from Pain**

*The Drukhari are experts in precise torture, sharp blades, cruel poisons and eldritch abominations. They prey upon worlds, surging from hidden portals unknown to those they see merely as cattle or playthings. As the Drukhari feed on the souls of the slain, they become imbued with supernatural might, eventually turning into killing machines.*

When running Drukhari, the GM gains Pain as follows:

- In combat, after rolling initiative, depending on encounter difficulty.

**Trivial** 1 Pain

**Low** 2 Pain

**Moderate** 3 Pain

**Severe** 4 Pain

**Extreme** 5 Pain

- Each time one of the Drukhari's enemies is reduced to 0 hit points: 1 Pain.
- Each time one of the Drukhari's enemies gains the Frightened condition: 1 Pain.

Use a stack of tokens that are visible to all players to track available Pain.

**Empower** (free action) At the start of its turn, the GM can choose to spend 1 Pain to empower any drukhari creature, giving it a +1 status bonus to attack rolls, damage rolls, saves against fear effects, and a +10-foot status bonus to all Speeds for 1 round.

This is the same [[Raider]] stat block introduced in *When Elves Attack*, reused here for the Drukhari appendix.
