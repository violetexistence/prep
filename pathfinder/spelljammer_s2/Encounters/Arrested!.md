# Arrested!

During this adventure, the dottari try to arrest the PCs. If the authorities manage to drag off any of the PCs, they're brought to Dhoffram Keep, one of the fortresses in Corentyn. Linetta Seacarver springs the PCs from their cells before the end of the night. Choose a condition as part of the escape:

- Lose a valuable piece of equipment.
- …and that item resurfaces on the black market / in someone else's hands.
- An allied faction is displeased.
- Gain a lasting injury or trauma.
- Someone else pays the price.
- Information soon relied upon goes stale.
