**Talking to the Staff**

There is a 50% chance that any of the staff at the restaurant remember Harley by name, but 100% chance if accurately described in any way. The staff can randomly recall any or all of the items from the table below. **Era** (*stylish non-binary android negotiator*), the bartender tonight, was Harley’s main contact here and they are the one who was charged with delivering her message and her “gift”.

1. Harley visited Man-O-War once or twice a year (true).
2. Always brought a different crew, 4-6 diverse people (true).
3. Always spent big. One famed bill came to over 5,000 credits (false, it was less than 1,200 cr).
4. She is an Veskari agent sent to spy on Prince Andru. (false)
5. She is the secret paramour of Prince Andru. (false)
6. Era, the bartender, has the hook up to private parties on her yacht, the Estrexia. (true)

**Era** and Harley have a business relationship. Era procures things on and around Bral and in return Harley introduces Era to her associates. As a result the android has accumulated one of the best lists of contact on the rock. They are fond of Harley and are genuinely sad to hear of her death. Era always knew that Harley’s big personality was a mask and that there was a deeper need that drove her. Era would have like to know her better. They are willing to volunteer any information that might be helpful, but will not actively participate in a mission.
