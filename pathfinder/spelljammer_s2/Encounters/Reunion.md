# Reunion

The streets here aren’t like elsewhere on the Rock of Bral. Foot traffic is minimal – silent couriers and stern guards mostly – and only a handful of gleaming corporate sedans carry their chauffeured passengers home for the evening. The lights of Starhaven, Prince Andru’s personal residence, gleam from distant heights, while all around you the gated estates of a dozen noble villas each more luxurious than the last lie stitched across High City in an effort to simultaneously obstruct clear street views of their grounds and avoid any obstruction of their own view of Lake Bral.

By comparison, the entrance to Man-O-War looks jarringly out of place. The tasteful, neon glow of the sign above the door assures you they are open, but the approach is a chaotic gauntlet of urban decay. The footpath is completely overrun by skeletal scaffolding and flapping, translucent plastic. Half the street is torn apart, revealing the city's guts; safety cones ring open sewer hatches like plastic sentinels. Industrial drones engage in an intricate, screeching dance of cutting beams and heavy roller treads. The mechanical noise is considerable, a grinding contrast to the quiet opulence of the neighboring estates.

Not until you cross the threshold and seal the heavy door behind you does the lo-fi synthwave reach your ears, instantly muting the industrial din outside. The interior is designed to feel like the intimate below-deck of an archaic warship. Instead of hammocks and coiled lines, there are silk drapes and polished darkwood tables. Warm light glows from hanging oil lamps and a few strategically placed circular fish tanks, casting dancing shadows across the room.

A lounge is visible from the entrance where a well-heeled bartender glances over at you momentarily. Lines of electroluminescent synthskin shimmer along their cheeks and jawline, pulsing gently with the music. They give you a small, knowing smile before turning back to casual conversation with another patron seated at the bar.

Before you, a flickering scrib is mounted atop a waist-high pedestal. A list of upcoming reservations scrolls from top to bottom on its screen in a crisp, azure font. Also offered is the silent, glowing option to enter your name.

**Enter your name.**

A PC who enters their real name is recognized and checked-in successfully. 

**Scan the reservations.**

Nestled in the scrolling list is *A. Harley – Captain’s Table*. It shows 0 guests checked in. Check-in can be done anonymously by tapping any of the reservations.

**Once checked-in.** 

The kiosk offers an option to be seated or take a beverage in the lounge and be notified when more of your party has arrived. Either choice results in powered floor lighting guiding the PC to their destination – either into an empty table in the lounge or past the lounge and through several corridors and hatches to the Captain’s Table.

**Lounge.**

Era (stylish non-binary android fixer) is working the lounge tonight. They will see that anyone in her area is taken care of and engage in polite conversation. Successful **DC 15 Sense Motive** – Era is unusually familiar, even for a bartender. Her eyes shine with recognition. If the PCs reveal they are associates of Harley, Era’s suspicions are confirmed. They readily engage in conversation about their mutual acquaintance.

**Captain’s Table.**

This table is located at “stern of the ship” with windows offering a panoramic view of the ocean (Lake Bral). A tasteful selection of archaic navigational tools mounted to the wall along with a pair of maps – one of Bral’s Underside as well as a starchart labeled “Koronus Expanse”.
