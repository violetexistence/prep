# Up and Away

Standing on the deck of Revelation, you see scores of other ships transiting the Strait, some of their sails bending to propel them only across the surface of the ocean, and others like yours whose magical helm lifts them up into the sky and through the clouds. You find yourself sailing through a void filled with streaks of color and blinking stars. A school of space-dwelling fish swims past the ship on the starboard side.
