# Getaway

- At the end of 5 rounds of combat or **Ravenor** and the Thoughtform Summons are defeated, alarms blare throughout the streets. DC 25 Recall Knowledge (Legal Lore, Warfare Lore, Society) to determine that reinforcements will arrive in 1 minute.
- This scene uses the **Chase** subsystem to determine whether the PCs can pull off a successful getaway. Each round of the chase represents 1 minute. The dottari reinforcements appear at the first obstacle in the second round of the chase, move on initiative count 10, advance automatically at the rate of one obstacle per round.
- If the dotarri are ever on the same obstacle as the PCs, run an encounter with 2 elite [excruciator divisions](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=4823&Elite=true&NoRedirect=1). If the dottari defeat the PCs, they're brought to a local jail in Dhoffram Keep (see ***Arrested!***).
- **Linetta Seacarver** (opportunistic female human witch) is captain of the *Revelation*—a galleon class spelljammer—anchored in the harbor. Linetta has been well paid to help the PCs get off world, and has designated a non-descript warehouse in the [district] as a rendezvous point. She has an animal companion hiding in the alley near the museum to make contact with the PCs.
- **Silhouette** (vengeful female halfling rogue) and **Zenfiro** (defiant male human archivist) can also assist in the getaway, if they are with the PCs.

**WHISPER IN THE ALLEY**        **OBSTACLE 10**

**Chase Points** 1; **Overcome** DC 27 Perception to notice Grackle leading them down a side passage, DC 29 Society to choose the best path to escape

![[grackle_reference.png]]

An alley cuts away from the Museum of History, where an orange-throated blackbird waits to intercept the PCs. The bird, Grackle, is able to speak and warns them that it's time to run–the dottari will surely be looking to arrest them. Grackle (or whoever is speaking though him) knows a safe location, so they should follow him, but they'll have to make their way through several neighborhoods to the Warehouse District.

**THROUGH HIGH QUARTER STREETS**      **OBSTACLE 10**

**Chase Points** 3; **Overcome** DC 25 Deception to blend in with the High Quarter crowds, DC 29 Religion to locate hidden shrines that offer temporary sanctuary, DC 27 Stealth to sneak around the cramped buildings.

*The alley dumps you straight into High Quarter, steep switchback streets with plenty of cover but the wrong kind of crowded. Tradespeople and laborers going about their work, civil servants mingling among commoners, and dottari protecting them. No one goes about their business in haste which means extra eyes on anyone moving with purpose.*

**Grackle** *Caw! Caw! Get to the old dividing wall. Fly east. Fly casual. Caw!*

**Religion** (trained) Circumspect shrines hide in plain sight, disguised as parts of shopfronts and studios.

**PAST THE WALL**          **OBSTACLE 10**

**Chase Points** 3; **Overcome** DC 27 Athletics to scale the wall, DC 27 Crafting to exploit a crumbling wall section, DC 29 Deception or Stealth to evade the dottari's notice.

*The street runs out against Corentyn's old dividing wall, the one separating the city's western half from its east. There are plenty of passages through, where citizens are free to cross, but all the typical ones swarm with dottari.*

**Grackle** *Caw! Caw! This part's easy. Dottari don't fly. Caw!*

**Crafting** (trained) The wall is ancient and crumbling. Several less typical passages have been sealed for the safety of Corentyn's citizens.

**EASTTOWN STROLL**         **OBSTACLE 10**

**Chase Points** 2; **Overcome** DC 29 Diplomacy to charm any observers, DC 27 Society to move with the confidence of well-off residents.

*Rows of cozy houses and wealthy estates are nestled on the gentle slopes of Easttown. It's a relief from the press and grades on the west side of the wall. Lavender and rosemary find your nostrils while playful laughter reaches your ears. Nearby, a couple strolls arm in arm past a woman in a wide brimmed hat deadheading roses.*

**Grackle** *Pee ewe – I smell a rat. Less garbage on the beach. Caw!*

**Society** (expert) A subtle game of status is played by the residents here. Each exchange of rumor and gossip first acknowledges the identity and rank of its participants, meaning—strangers are marked and probably reported.

**SHORELINE SPRINT**         **OBSTACLE 10**

**Chase Points** 3; **Overcome** DC 29 Acrobatics to nimbly move through the mire, DC 27 Nature to observe the quality of the sand and determine firm footholds, DC 25 Survival to follow a set of tracks across the sand

*Easttown is behind, ahead the mainland shore of a narrows. Across is the Jut, a mile-long spit sheltering Corentyn from the booming surf of Hespereth Strait. On this side, wind-blown grasses and Aleppo pines give way to acres of sea-washed sand and tidepools reaching north to south. No houses are built here. No watch towers and no dottari in sight.*

**Grackle** *Caw! Caw! Two wings is better than none. Follow south. Drenches await. Caw!*

**Survival** (trained) The sand is extremely unstable. While there is no one here at present, some do take this path with enough regularity that their tracks remain.

**EAST DRENCHES PASSAGE**        **OBSTACLE 10**

**Chase Points** 2; **Overcome** DC 29 Athletics to climb along the side of the bridge, DC 27 Intimidation to push through sightseers on the bridge, DC 25 Performance to steal a canal boat and pass as one of Corentyn's singing ferriers.

*The shoreline runs out at the canal separating East Drenches from the Warehouse District beyond, and East Drenches does not do subtle — it's cafes with fewer than ten seats, plein air painters, and a shopkeeper-heavy street that watches for improper dress the same way Easttown watches for strangers. The bridge is the fastest way across, but it's also full of the neighborhood's daily foot traffic, out enjoying their unhurried lives.*

**Grackle** *Over the bridge. Caw! Through the woods. Caw!*

**Special** A PC can hire a boat to gain a +2 circumstance bonus to their check (10 gp), automatically succeed (50 gp), or critically succeed (100 gp).

**Performance** (trained) One of the canal boats is already full of impatiently waiting passengers, who are looking around for their ferrier, who is nowhere to be found.

**WAREHOUSE DISTRICT MAZE**       **OBSTACLE 10**

**Chase Points** 2; **Overcome** DC 25 Diplomacy to ask directions, DC 27 Perception to read nearly illegible sign posts or spot logos and landmarks DC 27 Survival to navigate the twisting streets and narrow alleys by feel

*The major lanes in the Warehouse District are packed with lines of dockside teamsters and city porters waiting to deliver freight, while minor alleys remain empty. The signposts here were never meant for people trying not to be found, and the same cramped geometry that swallows sightlines also swallows sense of direction.*

**Grackle** *This way. No, not that way. Caw! (echoing somewhere nearby) Caw!*

**Underworld Lore, Mercantile Lore** (trained) The Falcon Shipping Manifest Company went out of business long ago, but the falcon's talons in their logo can still be seen on one in every three buildings in this district. Local underworld meetups often use these strategically preserved logos as a kind of pointer toward other covert signs.

**LOSING THE TAIL**         **OBSTACLE 10**

**Chase Points** 3; **Overcome** DC 27 Deception to distract the tail from following, DC 29 Perception to watch for when the tail isn't paying attention and slip away, DC 25 Stealth to avoid being spotted while entering the safe house

*Near the safe house, a building with a peeling mural of the idealized grandmother, Grackle turns away from his route and zooms away through a back alley.*

**Grackle** *Caw! Caw! We're being tailed. Split up. Don't let anyone see you enter. Caw!*

**Special** Start with 1 Chase Point for every obstacle between the PCs and their pursuers. If the PCs don't split up, increase the DCs of their skill checks to lose their tails by 2. Splitting up means PCs can't take the Aid action—they will need to make these checks on their own.

**Reward:** Grant the PCs 120 XP the first time they arrive at Linetta's safe house.
