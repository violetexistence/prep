# When Elves Attack

The ship slows as another vessel comes into view, approaching the Revelation from astern. The pursuing vessel looks like someone took a knife and carved a single-masted pleasure sloop out of it. You can just make out figures on the other vessel's deck scrambling to arm the ship's forward cannon.

![[drukhari_raider_ship.png]]

"Aeldari," curses Captain Seacarver, "and they don't look friendly. Sodding bold of them punch above their class, like this—they don't know we don't have the hands to fire all our guns. All the same, better prepare yourselves for a fight."

The "aeldari" ship is *Bloodwine*, a Drukhari raider. It's crewed by trueborn kabalites of the Black Heart. **Dracon Azmadian** and his sisters **Zanth** and **Jalyn** prepare a boarding action on the Bloodwine's swept fairings. A Nadirist priest of the Haemonculi, **Alturi**, is at the helm. They keep nine plasmoids in thrall as additional crew.

![[Raider]]

These kabalites have been ordered to maintain their distance from Bral and not to in any way spoil the surprise of the upcoming raid. However, these ones feel such orders are for the halfborn. They know the hurt privateers have put on Vect's ships in Pactspace and they intend on currying favor with their beloved Archon with an early birthday present—Seacarver's head.
