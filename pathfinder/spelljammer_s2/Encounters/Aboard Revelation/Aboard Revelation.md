# Aboard Revelation

**The Crew** The only crew other than [[Linetta Seacarver|Linetta]] is a spelljamming flumph, named [[Flapjack]].

**The Cargo** There are 20 mules stabled in the main cargo deck giving [[Linetta Seacarver|Linetta]] the appearance of a legitimate trader. Their feed in the lower steerage deck conceals her real prize—stolen siege engine parts from Corentyn. She intends to bring these to Prince Andru in return for an overhaul in the shipyard and the prince's support in rescuing her crew from the Graveworlds.

**The Course** Captain [[Linetta Seacarver|Seacarver]] gives orders to make best speed for the Belt, a protective field which is sure to discourage any ships from following. It is 1d6+2 days travel to the Belt and another 1d6 days to Bral, which is located inside the Belt. On Bral, Harley has arranged for the transfer of Silhouette and any other assets recovered from Operation: Broken Arch. The PCs' own ship, *Destiny*, is in dock on the Rock of Bral.

**[[Linetta Seacarver]]** is a privateer captain with a letter of marque from Prince Andru of Bral. When she is not destroying or taking as prize ships belonging to the Prince's enemies, she operates as smuggler, spy, and occasionally a legitimate trader.

**[[Flapjack]] the Flumph** serves as Linetta's helmsman and pilot, and is one of only two people in the 'verse she truly trusts.

**Traevus and Gang** If [[Traevus]] has come along, he and his gang pose as "passengers" to any outsiders. [[Traevus]] himself doesn't care about gold.

1. **Rope and Anchor Storage** Accessible by climbing down a ladder down from the forecastle, this is where the ship's anchors are weighed. It's also where spare lines for the over 26 miles of rigging are kept. Stashed in a secret compartment inside the capstan is a set of artist's charcoal and notebook.

**Charcoals** These appear to be ordinary gray/black charcoals. However, when held they impart to surfaces whatever color the holder imagines.

**Notebook** Full of vividly colored charcoal drawings. Some are impressionist scenes of Pactspace as seen from the deck. Others are portraits of the crew.

- Screaming lashunta with delicate antennae, green mohawk, furiously manifesting a kind of psychic shield (**Raia**).
- Gleeful ysoki, goggles, face bathed in reflected energy, looking over a table covered in tools and disassembled constructs (**Quig**)
- Stoic kasatha, head wrapped in flowing scarf, eyes closed, meditating with sunlight on face (**Altronus**)
- Heavily muscled, tattooed, vesk warrior looking up from plate, laughing, face covered in mashed potato, tank top with MMAC logo (**Obozaya**)

2. **Stateroom** Designed for use by those booking high passage, several pieces of fine hardwood furnishings are provided—incuding a bedframe (no mattress, stacked with boxes), writing desk (a tooth in the ink well), mirror (covered with oil-stained drop cloth), commode with artistic privacy screen, and a small copper tub (filled with bags of expired snack chips).

**Boxes** Each of the boxes is filled with clothing and various personal effects. There is a name on each box—some names have more than one box: Raia, Quig, Altronus, Obozaya, Iseph

**Tooth** Object Reading (1st) will show that a cleaning crew that came aboard a month ago found this and placed it here awkwardly, not wanting to ask about its provenance. Object Reading (4th) or asking [[Linetta Seacarver|Linetta]] reveals this is her tooth, knocked out during a vigorous sparring match/foreplay with Obo. She wore a chain around her neck until the chain broke about a year ago. She replaced the tooth with a gold version for privateer vibes.

3. **Captain's Quarters** Furnished similar to the stateroom, except no privacy screen and there is an actual mattress on the bed. [[Linetta Seacarver|Linetta]] keeps a spare hat on a rack on the wall. Several fixtures are mounted high on the walls and hung from the ceiling, which appear to be a bird habitat. There is a dreamy charcoal drawing of a powerfully built Vesk warrior on the backside of her door.

**Desk** A log is kept locked (4 successes, DC 25 Thievery) in one of the drawers. [[Linetta Seacarver|Linetta]] keeps the key on her.

**Picture** This is also an Iseph original. Obo consented to this sentimental depiction of herself on one condition: that [[Linetta Seacarver|Linetta]] kept it where the crew would not see it.

**Habitat** This is used by Grackle. It's dotted with stains from berries and covered in snack chip crumbs.

4. **Galley** The door to this area is split into an upper and lower half. The Upper half can be opened while the lower half if kept closed to dish up hot food on the plates of passers by. It's cramped in this room, but just enough room for a cook stove, firewood, and a cook to work.

**Stove** There is a superb repair toolkit that has fallen behind the stove. It was used by the ship's last cook to repair the chimney shortly before they were captured. Anyone searching will find it with a secret DC 20 Perception check.

5. **Spelljammer Helm** The door to this room is reinforced and can be locked from the inside. It bears several old scorch marks and marks from boarding axes. Inside, navigation tables and charts surround an ornate throne. There is no window in this compartment.

**Throne** This is a major spelljammer helm, the primary motive force both in wildspace and in atmosphere. It requires investment (sitting on it) by a creature capable of casting spells allowing the caster to pilot the ship. For the full rules, see [Spelljamming in Pf2e](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1DAESe1oa6JTZu17qn7nwdSYHCmvzz2ga3JJ6lHbCAJ4/edit#heading=h.4zgj0biqefm2). [[Flapjack]] is the typical occupant of this room when the ship is under way.

**Charts** [[Flapjack]] and [[Linetta Seacarver|Linetta]] have a half dozen different routes mapped into and out of a Pactspace world named Eox. Each is marked with two notations: time and one to five skulls. The skulls denote danger level.

6. **Spelljammer's Quarters** When there was a full crew, these were [[Flapjack|Flapjacks]]' private quarters. Now, he spends little time here, instead sleeping in Iseph's bunk most of the time. There is a large hive-like structure attached to the ceiling, made of woven material. The floor is a mess, strewn with detritus and overturned furniture. A large bay window affords a stunning view port side and aft of the ship.

7. **Mess Hall** Two long tables and benches and one smaller round table with chairs take up most of this compartment. A large bay window affords a stunning view starboard and aft of the ship. The tables are remarkably clean, if a bit dusty, as if they have not been used in a while. A large document is framed and hangs on the wall next to an advert for Oteos and a travel agency offering excursion to Phlogiston Utopia.

**Document** This is a charter signed by the founding crew members and dates back to six years ago. It specifies profit sharing by job and tenure. It outlines the number of hours in a day each member is obligated to stand duty and the privileges to which they are entitled. It also specifies the process by which grievances are redressed with the captain. It is signed by **Raia Danviri** (engineer), **Keskodai** (surgeon), **Iseph** (bosun), **Obozaya Ymeros** (sergeant-at-arms), **Quig Dexel** (ship's carpenter), **Altronus Barasul Dovenayan** (quartermaster), **[[Flapjack]]** (pilot), and finally by **[[Linetta Seacarver]]** (shipmaster).

8. **Galley** Large counter height tables strewn with dishes in various states of cleanliness accompanied by a few rats, who scatter on approach. There are racks of knives and other cooking tools. One such is a species of multi-function device with articulated arms attached to a combination mixing bowl toaster coffee maker, which sits in a lake sticky looking batter. A cabinet near the door is covered in frost and has a number of corks strewn on the floor nearby.

**Cabinet** This is a magical storage device which keeps up to bulk 10 worth of items cold. It is currently stocked with cold brew coffee (Bulk 3) and irish cream (Bulk 1). Also, a [potion of quickness](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2950), [greater antiplague](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=3297) x2, [moderate elixir of life](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=3308) x4, and an [expanded healer's toolkit](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2727) are stored here.

**Brunch** The multi-function kitchen appliance is one of Quig's creations, called Portable Chef. It has not been working well for a few weeks now, turning [[Flapjack]]'s last attempt at cooking into a disaster, which is still in evidence. It can serve as cookware and attempt Cooking Lore +16 checks on its own to prepare meals as well as alchemical food, but only from its limited menu. It's also currently in sore need of repair. Until it is repaired, all cooking checks made with portable chef have outcomes reduced one level. Some of the items on the menu are marked with an initial—indicating the crew member whose favorite meal this is.

9. **Crew Quarters** Bunks stacked two high with and a shared closet compose the standard crew accommodations. The two compartments in the fore of the ship have beds for six. The aft quarters only have beds for four and have windows, where the fore compartments have none. The aft compartments have more space per occupant but the one to fore smell considerably better most days. The compartment amidship is a converted workshop.

11. **Companionway** This compartment is the location of the ship's locker and a dozen other storage cabinets, which can be accessed by removing panels from the floor and the benches that line the walls. This is the usual location of crew meetings and is used by off duty crew to relax and socialize. Backsides of floor panels have games, bettings odds for various pools are posted on the stairwell.

12. **Upper Cargo Hold** Currently, this hold is the home of 20 mules, all secured in a makeshift pen which has been hastily built and it is unlikely to hold them for the duration. This is also the gun deck. Six cannon are stowed here (three on each side), each of which has been incorporated into the mule pen as load bearing elements of the fence. There is a large hatch on the ceiling and on the deck, leading up to the main deck and steerage respectively.

13. **Lower Cargo Hold** Currently, this hold is filled with enormous feed bins, meant to supply the mules with sustenance during the journey. More than cursory analysis might suggest this is far too much feed for 20 mules on a 10 day voyage. In fact, the bins are mostly full of siege equipment, stolen from Corentyn, and thinly covered by the feed.

14. **Brig** Sanctioned crew and other prisoners are locked here. The compartment has no windows. The door to the brig is lockable from the outside. It leads to a small compartment meant for a guard to stand watch over the cage. Another door, also lockable from the outside, separates the guard post from the cage. Neither door is currently locked and there are no occupants of the brig. It is being used as storage.

15. **Pantry** The pantry is reasonably well stocked for a ship operating with a skeleton crew. Closer inspection reveals about half the stores are well expired. Mostly canned goods and bulk grains. A root cellar of sorts is also growing some interesting vegetable evolutions.
