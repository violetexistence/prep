# Spelljamming in Pf2e

**SPELLJAMMING HELM** (rare, invested)       ITEM 5+

**Usage** Installed aboard a ship

**Price** by type

Installing a spelljamming helm is a process that requires 1 day and a successful DC 20 skill check using the skill associated with the tradition used to create the helm—Arcana, Nature, Occultism, or Religion.

Investment is typically done by sitting at the helm in chair form, but not always. Spelljamming helms can also take the form of crowns, gloves, and other worn items. This must be done by a creature capable of *spellcasting*.

This device allows a spellcaster to impart motive force to a ship on world, in wildspace, and on the astral sea. The ship gains an item bonus to its speed based on the highest rank spell the pilot can cast.

While invested, pilots often report a feeling of being in water up to their neck. They can use their senses as if standing anywhere on the ship.
