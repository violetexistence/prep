## Episodes

1. [[Night at the Museum]] — cold open: a raid on the Corentyn Museum of History, and the extraction of Silhouette.
2. [[Getaway]] — a Chase across Corentyn, led by Grackle, to Linetta's safehouse.
3. [[Arrested!]] — fallback consequences if the dottari catch the party.
4. [[Safehouse]] — Traevus's crew wants their gold before the *Revelation* can leave port.
5. [[Up and Away]] — Revelation lifts off from Corentyn and into wildspace.
6. [[Aboard Revelation]] — the ship itself, deck by deck, and its crew.
7. [[When Elves Attack]] — the Drukhari raider *Bloodwine* runs the party down.
8. [[The Fall of Bral]] — Vect's corsair fleet raids the Rock of Bral, and the Drukhari appendix (Children of Thorns, the wych cult, Aeldari Soul Lore).
9. [[Reunion]] — Man-O-War, and Harley's reserved table.
10. [[Harley's Message]] — her final recorded words to the Angels.
