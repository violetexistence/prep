# Flapjack

Helmsman of [[Aboard Revelation]].

Flapjack was living in a crate of rotting produce at the end of an abandoned dock, when he met [[Linetta Seacarver|Linetta]]. She walked into his world crying tears of anger and frustration. For some strange reason, Flapjack chose that moment to open his crate and say hello, despite being terrified of being perceived. After listening to the girl’s story, he learned that she had brilliant plans and terrible judgment in friends—which had cost all her coin. This was a problem he could solve!

> The only thing worse than being sad is being sad and alone.

Their first plan was to make back enough money for two tickets to the all-you-can-eat breakfast buffet at Chinwag & Chew. The heist involved the auction of hulk Hurrah, a Mop, and a Rum Cull from Gifftown. By the time they had their fill of burnt bacon, honey-butter cream porridge, and pancakes, they resolved to always be the cutters in this world, and never again the conies.
