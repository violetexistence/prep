# Traevus

Enforcer working for [[Linetta Seacarver]]; confronts her at the [[Safehouse]] over unpaid wages, and may later try to seize [[Aboard Revelation|Revelation]] for himself.

A dashing corsair captain sporting a feathered hat, leather baldric, and surrounded by a half dozen rough looking enforcers, smiles as you come into the light.

“You made it. Incredibly clean work back there with the dottari. Seeing you give those bootlickers the laugh warms my heart… right—no sense delaying our departure, eh? Let’s shove off…”

“Not so fast, Seacarver. Where’s our gold?” interjects one of her enforcers.

The corsair captain rolls her eyes casually, “Really, Traevus. You made plenty, already. Now is not the time to get greedy.”

Traevus growls and steps menacingly toward her, leveling a pistol waist height. “You’re fixing to peel after we bled and good? Pay us what’s owed… or feed the wyrm.”

[[Linetta Seacarver|Seacarver]] gestures with open hands, “Hey, hey, no need to get nasty. If you really feel that way, I’m sure we can work something out. This is me—I’m good for it…”

The sound of the hammer being cocked back is the only reply—along with an I dare you sneer. The corsair captain shifts her gaze to you and raises an eyebrow.

If Traevus has come along, he and his gang pose as “passengers” to any outsiders. Traevus himself doesn’t care about gold. What he cares about is respect. He uses the gold to earn respect. Nobody follows a leader they don’t respect and what people respect is someone who can make them rich.

If Traevus spends much time aboard the *[[Aboard Revelation|Revelation]]*, he’s going to see that ships are a great way to make more gold than any one job. Soon, he’ll decide to just take it. The members of his gang won’t argue with the logic, but they will have to decide if they’d rather crew for Traevus as captain or crew for Seacarver—and eliminate Traevus. They won’t jump ship unless they know they have it better with Seacarver. The PCs will soon have two chances to influence this decision, by how they use the “passengers” in upcoming encounters.
