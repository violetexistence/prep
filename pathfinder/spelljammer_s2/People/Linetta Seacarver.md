# Linetta Seacarver

![[linetta_seacarver.png]]

Captain of [[Aboard Revelation]].

Linetta Seacarver is a privateer captain with a letter of marque from Prince Andru of Bral. When she is not destroying or taking as prize ships belonging to the Prince’s enemies, she operates as smuggler, spy, and occasionally a legitimate trader.

Linetta grew up in the belt. On the streets she learned to survive on her wits and the value of a true friend. Many might call themselves friends of hers (due to her dashing charm), but there are only two people in the ‘verse Linetta trusts. One of those is her helmsman, [[Flapjack]]—the only person who can beat her at cards. The other is Harley.

A few years back, a rare case of conscience got Linetta in trouble with an employer. She walked away from a job in the Graveworlds only to be captured shortly after by the corpsefolk union she never robbed. It was clear to Harley upon receiving a tip from her informants, the ethical pirate Seacarver was. Angels were able to spring Linetta before the worst happened to her, but not before it happened to many of her crew.

These days Linetta doesn’t take work until she knows the politics.

## Captain's Quarters

Furnished similar to the stateroom, except no privacy screen and there is an actual mattress on the bed. Linetta keeps a spare hat on a rack on the wall. Several fixtures are mounted high on the walls and hung from the ceiling, which appear to be a bird habitat. There is a dreamy charcoal drawing of a powerfully built Vesk warrior on the backside of her door.

**Desk** A log is kept locked (4 successes, DC 25 Thievery) in one of the drawers. Linetta keeps the key on her.

**Picture** This is also an Iseph original. Obo consented to this sentimental depiction of herself on one condition: that Linetta kept it where the crew would not see it.

**Habitat** This is used by Grackle. It’s dotted with stains from berries and covered in snack chip crumbs.
